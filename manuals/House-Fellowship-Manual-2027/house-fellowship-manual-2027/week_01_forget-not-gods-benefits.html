Week 01 — THANKSGIVING SUNDAY, 6TH SEPTEMBER 2026

FORGET NOT GOD'S BENEFITS

Memory Verse
“Bless the Lord, O my soul, and forget not all His benefits”. Psalm 103:2.
Bible Text
Psalm 103:1-22
Introduction
The Psalmist reminded us in Psalm 103 of God's provisions for humanity that should not be winked at, starting with privileges of waking up daily, getting air not being paid for, the human anatomy working effectively, and deliverance from wicked forces and their antics, anyone that rules out this awesome kindness of God should be reminded of John 15:4 that without Him, we are nothing. We should appreciate the Almighty God for not keeping us in darkness, always being prompt in yielding to our daily request and making sure we are not abandoned.
Lesson Outline One
GOD'S UNDESERVED BENEFITS
Undeserved benefits refer to all good things God gives us freely, not because we earned them or deserve them, but because of His love, mercy and grace. They can be termed “grace blessings, unmerited favour”. Daily Provision every day, God unleashes to our paths food, shelter, resources - Matthew 6:26. Peace of mind - Philippians 4:7. Deliverance from wicked forces - Psalm 91:3. Sound health and healing. Psalm 103:3, 3 John 1:2 His Love and Mercy. John 3:16, Lamentations 3:22-23. Access to God's presence and gratification of requests. Hebrews 4:16, Jeremiah 32:27
Lesson Outline Two
TO LIVE AND THANK GOD
When you consider Samson and David, their exploits, longevity, quickness to divine response to their requests, it can be traced to life of gratitude. David had fulfilling life because he knew how to appreciate God for every blessing received, promises fulfilled, victory accessed, deliverance received and enemies defeated. David knew where he came from, he understood victory is not by might or armoury, 1 Samuel 17. He also knew that getting success in every endeavor comes from God's love and His abiding presence. To live and be thankful means: a. Being victorious, successful and productive: b. Getting timely response from God: c. Being repositioned to enviable heights: d. Being guided and supported: and e. Being perpetually in thanksgiving.
Conclusion
We are all sinners by nature - Romans 3:23, we could never earn God's favour by our own works, it is only through Grace of God, His unearned kindness that is the reason we should show Him gratitude for His goodness, remember we are nothing without God's grace. You also need to extend love and mercy to others, as God has shown to us.
Prayer Points
  • Father, I thank You for all Your benefits in my life, seen and unseen, known and unknown.
  • Lord, deliver me from spiritual forgetfulness and ingratitude in Jesus' Name.
  • O Lord, renew Your benefits in my life daily according to Your Word.
  • Lord, satisfy my mouth with good things and renew my strength like the eagles.
  • Lord, as I remember Your benefits, help me to walk in obedience to You.
