Week 02 — SUNDAY, 13TH SEPTEMBER 2026

REMEMBER LOT'S WIFE

Memory Verse
“Remember Lot's wife.” Luke 17:32.
Bible Text
Genesis 19:12-26
Introduction
Lot's wife may not be known in the bible for many reasons but her tragic end by becoming a pillar of salt has always brought people's attention to her attachment to worldly allures, covetousness and indiscipline, despite angels' strong warning to her family to flee the city and never to look back after God released destruction into Sodom and Gomorrah she disobeyed God and suffered the consequences.
Lesson Outline One
LESSONS FROM LOT'S WIFE
a. Never yield to partial obedience, it is dangerous. God said they sgould flee from the city and don't look back. b. Don't look back once you secure your redemption from the devil - Luke 9:62. c. Guide your heart away from the past - Proverbs 4:23. d. Any offer from the devil is a time bomb. e. Your foundation will be tested at every juncture in life. f. Her destruction did not stop his family progressing out of danger. g. Spiritual focus is essential.
Lesson Outline Two
BE FOCUSED
Lot's wife is a symbol of disobedience, divided loyalty and loss of spiritual focus. Though she left Sodom but her attachment to the beautiful things in that city was still engraved in her heart. a. Don't yield to the devil's temptation. Proverbs 1:10. b. Stay on God's direction not on the world's distraction. Solomon's heart went astray - 1 kings 11:1 c. Remember half obedience is disobedience. 1 Samuel 15:22 d. Though the past seems attractive, it is also destructive. e. Distraction will pull you back to old ways that are cancerous. 2 Timothy 4:10
Conclusion
Remember Lot's wife is a powerful statement to everyone in this Christian race, looking back to the past affiliation to worldliness may end one's journey abruptly. Never attach yourself to the past, don't turn back to what God has delivered you from - 2 Corinthians. 5:17.
Prayer Points
  • Father, deliver me from the spirit of looking back.
  • Father, give me grace to move forward and never return to my past.
  • Lord, fix my eyes on Jesus, the author and finisher of my faith.
  • Every force assigned against me to stop my progress, scatter In Jesus Name.
  • Father, let my faith remain strong in times of pressure and temptation.
