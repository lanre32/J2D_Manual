Week 03 — SUNDAY, 20TH SEPTEMBER 2026

THEN AND NOW

Memory Verse
“When the ruler of the feast had tasted the water that was made and knew not whence it was (but the servants which drew the water knew), the governor of the feat called the bridegroom” John 2:9.
Bible Text
John 2:1-9.
Introduction
The story of the wedding in Cana of Galilee tells us that an unrelenting attitude pays, it yields positive ending, but one major take home in that story is getting Jesus into your daily endeavor. When the wedding was drawing towards a shameful ending due to the exhaustion of resources, His presence changed the ridiculous tune to a miraculous melody. Your then full of wanting, defeat and shame, with Jesus in your now, His influence can rewrite your story to positive vibes.
Lesson Outline One
THEN, THERE WAS A PROBLEM
Wine ran out. Wine at the wedding reception represents Symbol of Joy and celebration, it means then joy and celebration ceased. Sign of love and unity, it means withdrawal of harmony and being replaced with bitterness. Toast of good wishes. It means enemy replaced it with bad wishes There was limitation of resources, insufficiency set in. There was no rescue from impending shame. There was interruption from having a good beginning. The Enemy brought interruption to divine order (be fruitful and multiply).
Lesson Outline Two
HOW TO CHANGE THEN TO NOW?
Then, wine stopped but divine leading salvaged the dreadful situation through the mother of our Lord Jesus who prompted Him to restore order to the calamitous situation. To change then to now; a. Obey divine instruction. Job 36:11 b. Seek for help, closed mouth is a closed destiny. 2 kings 4:1-7 c. Resist every evil plan to divine order. d. Get the transformative power. e. Invite Jesus to your situation and turn emptiness to abundance. Hebrews 13:8.
Conclusion
In John 2:9, Jesus showed miraculous transformation at the wedding in Cana, turning then (impending shame, reproach), with His power, to wine. This symbolizes God's power to bring the best blessings at the right time and the joy of His presence in one's life.
Prayer Points
  • Father, thank you for changing my story from then and now.
  • Father, let every negative then in my life be swallowed up by a glorious now.
  • Lord, I refuse to return to what You have already delivered me from.
  • Lord, please establish my new identity in Christ.
  • Father, sustain me so I will not fall back to my former ways.
