Week 04 — SUNDAY, 27TH SEPTEMBER 2026

GOD'S FAVOURITE

Memory Verse
“As it is written, Jacob have I loved, but Esau have I hated,” Romans 9:13
Bible Text
Numbers 12:6-8.
Introduction
Being God's favourite means you are divinely, most preferred above others: And being divinely chosen over others, everyone redeemed by the shed blood of Christ has been given access to divine inheritance and procurement of God's promises, that is why you must do everything in your capacity to be amongst God's favourite who is duly granted provision to enjoy divine benefits others are exempted from.
Lesson Outline One
WHO ARE GOD'S FAVOURITES?
God's favourites are Humble and teachable. Numbers 12:3. In deep intimacy with God. Moses's relationship with God is exceptionally special. Exodus 33:11. Conscious in preserving God's secrets and ensure perfect adherence to His instructions. Hebrews 3:5 Courageous and bold. Hebrews 11:24-26. Sacrifices earthly gains for divine purposes. Those that have deep compassion for God's people. Exodus 32:32. Patient and also endure. They depend on God totally. 2 Timothy 2:3 Vessels unto honour with servant - leader's heart. 2 Timothy 2:21
Lesson Outline Two
YOU CAN BECOME GOD'S FAVOURITE
To become God's favourite, you need to: Walk in obedience. Job 36:11. Be faithful in small things that are committed to you. Joseph was faithful even in the prison. Luke 19:17 Have a heart of intercession and positive attitude to defend divine cause. 1 Samuel 17:36 Seek God's will above your own. Elisha left all to follow Elijah. 1 kings 19:19-21 Be a deep worshipper. John 4:23-24 Serve others with joy, help those in need. Acts 9:36-42 Have a heart of gratitude. Luke 17:17-19, Psalm 103:1-14
Conclusion
To become God's favourite, cultivate a heart that God can trust, humble, being compassionate, faithful and deeply intimate with Him.
Prayer Points
  • Father, thank you for loving Daddy and Mummy G.O and counting them as Your own.
  • Lord, let Daddy & Mummy GO and the entire RCCG continue to enjoy your favour.
  • Lord, remove anything in my life that blocks me from enjoying your favour.
  • Lord, mark me for distinction by your favour.
  • Father, preserve me and my household as your favourite.
