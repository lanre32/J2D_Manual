Week 05 — THANKSGIVING SUNDAY, 4TH OCTOBER 2026

NOT BREAKING RANKS

Memory Verse
“They shall run like mighty men; they shall climb the wall like men of war, and they shall march everyone on his ways, and they shall not break their ranks” Joel 2:7
Bible Text
Joel 2:7-8.
Introduction
Looking at Joel 2:7-8, A disciple of Christ must be discipline, united, spiritually aligned and be obedient, staying in his or her God given assigned position, an army of Christ are always in right place, no weapon can stop them. God's army are always charged and ready to enforce divine order.
Lesson Outline One
DIVINE ORDER
The word divine order means; God's perfect, established arrangement. 1 Corinthians 14:40. Following God obediently in your steps, decisions and relationship, not human impulses. Not confusion. 1 Samuel 16. Samuel followed God's order in making choice of king for the Israelites. Elimination of disorder, unnecessary struggles. 1 Corinthians 14:33 SIGNS OF OPERATING IN DIVINE ORDER. Experiencing inner peace in decisions and operations. David rejected Saul's garment in fighting Goliath. Everything will fall in place without force or human's inputs. 2 Chronicles 20:1-20.Divine direction step-by-step. Relationships aligning with destiny. Esther followed Mordecai's instructions, she later got divine alignment.
Lesson Outline Two
AVOID SELF GOVERNMENT
Ruling your own life by your wisdom, desires and opinions instead of submitting to God's authority, God's Word and the Holy Spirit is dangerous, doing what is right in your own eyes, Judges 21:25 can bring disconnection from God's guidance, Proverbs 14:12, loss of divine protection, opening door to temptation e.g Samson. Self-government can also lead to poor decision making and regret and easy target to the enemy, therefore desist from it and be in God's favourite agenda.
Conclusion
Following divine order brings peace not anxiety, direction not confusion, progress not stagnation. It helps to avoid mistakes and wasted seasons and positions one for God's blessings and favour.
Prayer Points
  • Father, remove every spirit of pride and rebellion from my heart, and give me submissive spirit.
  • Lord, help Daddy & Mummy GO to remain in their assigned position.
  • Father, keep me away from distraction that can cause me to break ranks.
  • Lord, give me grace to honour and submit to godly authority without murmuring.
  • Father, align my steps, my decisions, and my actions with Your divine arrangement.
Golden Diet For The Week
Monday They shall run like mighty men, they shall not break their ranks. Joel 2:7-8.
Tuesday Let all things be done decently and in order. 1 Corinthians 14:40.
Wednesday Breaking ranks often begins with rejecting spiritual authority. Hebrews 13:17
Thursday Unity preserves strength, division weakens purpose. Psalm 133:1
Friday Breaking ranks is a rebellion in disguise. 1 Samuel 15:23
Saturday Pride is a major cause of breaking ranks. Proverbs 13:10.
Sunday Disobedience removes divine covering. Exodus 23:20-21.
