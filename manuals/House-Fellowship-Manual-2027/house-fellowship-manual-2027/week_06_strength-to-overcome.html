Week 06 — SUNDAY, 11TH OCTOBER, 2026

STRENGTH TO OVERCOME

Memory Verse
“He giveth power to the faint; and to them that have no might he increaseth strength” Isaiah 40:29
Bible Text
Isaiah 40:29-31
Introduction
As we go through the journey of life, enough strength is required, in Isaiah 40:29, bible says Almighty God is always ready to give power to the tired and worn out and strength to the weak, there is none that never suffer exhaustion but we need God's grace to recharge for next assignment. When going through ten virgin's story in Matthew 25:1-10, your readiness to meet the bridegroom is based on having extra in you.
Lesson Outline One
WAIT ON THE LORD
Waiting on the Lord means trusting, hoping and depending on God when it seems everything is silent and not working according to one's program. When answers are delayed, when inputs are not yielding results, when requests are turned down, when strength is disappearing, you need to wait. Waiting is an act of faith that God will do it. It brings renewal of hope, refreshing to your souls, energizing your inner stamina. When you wait: Mistakes are reduced. Self-confidence is terminated, trust in God is built. Proverbs 3:5-6 Spiritual transformation is triggered. Moses encountered God in Exodus 3 Demonic activities are exposed and tamed. Mordecai waited at the gate and exposed conspiracy against king Ahasuerus that led to his promotion.
Lesson Outline Two
DIVINE EMPOWERMENT
Every believer desires strength, speed, victory and positive results, but God has a system for empowerment i.e. waiting on the Lord. When you wait, you are renewed according to Isaiah 40:31, you are experiencing an exchange. In Acts 1:8, bible says getting power comes when you have encounter with the Holy Spirit, i.e. having God's presence with you, disciples waited at the upper room before being empowered, therefore you have to be prayerful, full of positive expectations, be addicted to worship, praising God and be rooted in the word of God, all these will bring empowerment. Divine empowerment brings; a. Spiritual and physical transformation. Acts 13:1-2 b. Boldness and operating in God's wisdom. Proverbs 28:1, Acts 6:8 c. Destruction of embargoes and evil agenda. Acts 12:3-9 d. Positive changes to lives around you. Acts 28:2-10 e. Establishment of your words being anointed with fire. 2 kings 1:8-15
Conclusion
Strength from God brings transformation from ordinary lives to state of having supernatural strength. When Holy Spirit empowers you, you will rise above fear, weakness and limitations and then you begin to do extraordinary things.
Prayer Points
  • Father, release supernatural strength upon me to overcome every challenge of life.
  • Lord, let Daddy & Mummy GO be strengthened and renewed for greater assignment.
  • Lord, grant me enduring strength to stand firm till the end.
  • I receive power to overcome and to finish well.
  • Father, silence every force assigned to drain my strength in Jesus name.
