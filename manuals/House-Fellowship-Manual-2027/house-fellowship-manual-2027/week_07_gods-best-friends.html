Week 07 — SUNDAY, 18TH OCTOBER 2026

GOD'S BEST FRIENDS

Memory Verse
“And the scripture was fulfilled which saith, Abraham believed God, and it was imputed unto him for righteousness: and he was called the Friend of God. James 2:23
Bible Text
Genesis 18:17-33
Introduction
It may surprise you to know that God has best friends, they are so close to Him that they know things about Him that no one else knows. They are people with experiential knowledge of God that most people don't have. They don't talk deeply on how much they know God so that people will not misunderstand them.
Lesson Outline One
KNOW THEIR ATTRIBUTES - GOD'S BEST FRIENDS
Abraham was one of God's best friends, he was so close to God that God said He could not do anything significant on earth without first informing him (Genesis 18:17). Moses was also one of God's best friends, he was so close to God that God Himself testified in Numbers 12:8, that He spoke to Moses mouth to mouth. Their attributes are: They always say 'yes' to the Lord's command. They had close relationship with God and saw other sides of God others never saw. They heard things from God that they never told others and made God change His mind. Gen.18:24-32. They are people of faith. Hebrews 11:5, 6, Romans 4:18, 19. They prioritize prayers over complains, they always stand for others in deep intercession. 1 Cor. 3:5-9. God's best friends are pruned by God till they are genuinely humble. John 15:2 Nobody can get close to God without going through intense discipline.
Lesson Outline Two
KNOW THEIR EXPLOITS - GOD'S BEST FRIENDS
God's best friends have long standing exploits that are yet to be dethroned such as: I. Parting of the red sea and safe crossing by the Israelites. Exodus 14:21 22. ii. Bringing water out of the rock. Exodus 17:6. iii. Shutting the mouth of lions. Daniel 6:22. iv. Reading the handwriting on the wall and seeing deep prophetic secrets. Daniel 5:12, Daniel 9-12 v. Multiplying oil for the widow, making an iron head to float. 2 kings 4:1-7, 2 kings 6:5-7. vi. Stopping rain from falling, calling down fire from heaven. 1 kings 17:1, 1 kings 18:36-38
Conclusion
Every person who became God's best friend walked in obedience, intimacy, purity and consistent fellowship. And these led to exploits - things beyond human ability.
Prayer Points
  • Father, reveal Your secrets to me and grant me insight into Your plans.
  • Father, remove every barrier between You and me and deepen my intimacy with You.
  • Lord, give me unwavering faith that pleases You and qualifies me as Your friend.
  • Father, help me to walk with You daily, never to drift from Your counsel.
  • Father, arise and defend me as Your friend against all oppositions.
