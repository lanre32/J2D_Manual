Week 08 — SUNDAY, 25TH OCTOBER 2026

LONG SUFFERING

Memory Verse
“The Lord is not slack concerning His promise, as some men count slackness; but is longsuffering to us-ward, not willing that any should perish but that all should come to repentance”- 2 Pet.3:9.
Bible Text
Ephesians 4:2
Introduction
Longsuffering means enduring injury, offense, delay or provocation without revenge. It is patience under pressure. It is the strength to keep loving and forgiving when quitting seems easier. This nature is in God: slow to anger, giving room for repentance. Longsuffering is not weakness; it is controlled strength. Every believer is called to reflect this character.
Lesson Outline One
WHY LONGSUFFERING?
Longsuffering is more than ordinary patience - it is patience with people especially those who hurt, disappoint, misunderstand, or oppose us. It helps a believer to control anger, avoid revenge, and respond with love rather than frustration. 1. It reveals the nature of God. (Psalm 86:15) 2. It preserves unity in families, church and relationships. (Prov. 15:1) 3. It helps our spiritual growth through trials. (James 1:3-4) 4. It is a silent evangelism - it speaks louder than words. (1 Pet. 2:19-23) 5. Because God patiently endured us first. (Rom. 2:4)
Lesson Outline Two
BENEFITS AND BIBLICAL EXAMPLES
Benefits: - Brings peace and emotional stability. - Restores relationships and prevents bitterness. - Breaks anger, revenge and malice. - Protects destinies from wrong decisions. - Has heavenly reward. (Heb. 6:12) Examples: - God endured Israel for generations. - Noah preached 120 years amid mockery. (Gen. 6-7) - Joseph endured betrayal and prison. (Gen. 50:20) - David spared Saul repeatedly. (1 Sam. 24) - Jesus endured the cross for us. (Isa. 53:7)
Conclusion
Longsuffering is proof of Christ in us. A believer without endurance cannot reflect Jesus fully. As God is patient with our weaknesses, we must extend the same grace to others - in our homes, offices, and church and to the lost. Longsuffering is not optional for believers; it is a kingdom lifestyle.
Prayer Points
  • Thank You Lord for Your patience toward me.
  • Father, produce longsuffering in me by Your Spirit.
  • Deliver me from anger and impatience.
  • Help me to forgive like Jesus.
  • Let my life reflect Your patience daily.
