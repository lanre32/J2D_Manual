RCCG House Fellowship Manual

Week 10 — SUNDAY, 8TH NOVEMBER, 2026

FAITHFUL IN LITTLE

Memory Verse
“And he said unto him, Well done, thou good servant: because thou hast been faithful in a very little, have thou authority over ten cities. Luke 19:17.
Bible Text
Luke 19:12-24
Introduction
The Almighty God tests with little before trusting us with much. Men celebrate results, God celebrates faithfulness. In Luke 19, Jesus shows that God's promotion in the kingdom is not accidental - it is the reward for faithfulness in seemingly small matters.
Lesson Outline One
FAITHFUL IN LITTLE
God will always trust in little before committing much into His children's hands. David was trusted in little assignment of taking care of his father's sheep and he succeeded. Are you committed to that little assignment God has given you? Little is the classroom of greatness. What is faithfulness? Doing the right thing when no one is watching. Being consistent when no one is watching, serving God when it seems unnoticed. Being diligent when the environment is discouraging. Heaven takes records of small daily obedience. Get going when others are surrendering to the dictates of the flesh. Proverbs 1:10. Not allowing the perilous time to swallow what God has assigned to you as divine tasks. Gehazi succumbed to the lust of the eyes, Achan died because of the things of the world. 1 John 2:15-17
Lesson Outline Two
CONSEQUENCES OF BEING FAITHFUL
Faithful in little shows your trust in God, your value for God's assignment. It brings divine promotion. David moved from being a shepherd boy to becoming king of Israel. It attracts more responsibility, more influence and more open doors. Faithful in little receives divine boosting in character, stewardship, integrity and consistency. It promotes greatness. Joseph assumed greatness through his act of faithfulness. It attracts more gains of resources, and opportunities.
Conclusion
God is watching what you do with the little in your hands, little doors open big palaces, and little obedience births big testimonies. David was called to play the instrument for king Saul, and without hesitation he did and gained access to God's mind.
Prayer Points
  • Father, as I remain faithful in little let my life qualify for divine enlargement and promotion.
  • Father, destroy every spirit of carelessness or negligence assigned to ruin my life.
  • Lord, help me to serve you joyfully and faithfully in every responsibility.
  • Father, deliver me from the spirit of despising small beginnings.
  • O God, grant me the grace to value every assignment You gave me.
