RCCG House Fellowship Manual

Week 11 — SUNDAY, 15TH NOVEMBER 2026

HEAVEN BOUND HOME

Memory Verse
“Therefore shall a man leave his father and his mother and shall cleave unto his wife: and they shall be one flesh.” Genesis 2:24
Bible Text
Genesis 2:24-25
Introduction
The world had never experienced the kind of darkness ravaging homes and destinies of men as we have it today. There is a massive resurgence of ungodliness in families, communities and lack of respect for the Creator of the universe. This must not continue in our families, we must end it. The simplest way of dealing with the darkness ravaging homes is the application of light, having heavenly bound, spirit filled members in each family.
Lesson Outline One
QUALITIES OF HEAVEN BOUND HOME
The attributes of a heaven- bound home are: Raising family altar on daily basis. Having undiluted passion for things of God. Creating solutions in difficult situations. Resisting ungodly association and unruly behavior. Making provision for dying, perishing souls, inviting others to the narrow way to heaven. John1:45 Taming demonic operations that can bring the spreading of darkness. Living a sacrificial life. Romans 12:1-2, 2 Corinthians 12:15 Onward movement soldiers that ensure unstoppable revival. Luke 9:62
Lesson Outline Two
EFFECTIVENESS OF GODLY HOME
A Christian home is born when two children of God come together in marriage. A Christian home will not happen just because you are anointed, it demands diligence, commitment and sacrifice. Requirements for godly home are rooted in: · God's guidance and direction. Proverbs 3:5-6. · Maintaining purity and sanctity of marriage. Hebrews 13:4 · Rejecting worldly influences, avoid the pursuit of worldly values. 1 John 2:15-17. · Upholding biblical values. 2 Corinthians 6:14. · Understanding each other's strengths and weaknesses. · Having effective communication · Having provision to forgive each other's flaws, not creating room for secrecy. Eph. 4:32, Gen. 2:25-26.
Conclusion
Matthew 5:14 says we are light. Every heaven -bound home should shine as light, shining brightly for others to see and glorify God in love and purity.
Prayer Points
  • Father, let our family be anchored on heavenly values.
  • Father, let righteousness, peace and joy in the Holy Ghost dwell in our home.
  • Lord, draw every member of my family to Your salvation.
  • Father, let decisions and resources in every family glorify your name.
  • Father, protect my home and family from every worldly influence thatcan hinder our journey to heaven.
