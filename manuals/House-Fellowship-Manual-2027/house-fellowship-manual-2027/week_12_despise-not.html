Week 12 — SUNDAY, 22ND NOVEMBER 2026

DESPISE NOT

Memory Verse
“Behold, happy is the man whom God correcteth: therefore, despise not thou the chastening of the Almighty : Job 5:17
Bible Text
Job 36:5-10
Introduction
Despise not is one of the key words in the scripture, This serves as a warning and it should not be treated as unimportant. It is a call to honour and value a person, an instruction, a gift, or situation. It should be taken seriously, it should not be despised, it may be a pointer to your deliverance.
Lesson Outline One
UNDERSTANDING 'DESPISE NOT'
God is sounding this information to everyone never to despise, ignore His divine order or instructions. Despise not means Never belittle God's word or godly counsel. Proverbs 1:10. Don't treat information from God's throne lightly. 1 Samuel: 12-18. Eli despised God's instruction. Never ignore values divine information carries. Joshua followed instruction given to him by God in bringing down the wall of Jericho. Joshua 6:2-21. Never consider little assignment insignificant.
Lesson Outline Two
DESPISE NOT WHAT OR WHO?
In the kingdom of God, greatness often begins in small, hidden, quiet ways. God rarely starts with the spectacular, He begins with seeds of little instructions and guidance, not forests; with whispers, not thunder. Despise not your small starts. David started as a shepherd boy and ended asa king. Despise not divine instructions, they are simple but produce miracles. 2 kings 4:1-7. Others like your stewards may be important on your way to the top. 2 kings 5:2-4, 14. The grace and values in you too. David, Joseph and Daniel never despised the gifts of God in them The chastening from the Lord. Proverbs 3:11 The poor or the needy. James 2:6. Romans 14:3 Despise not prophesyings. 1 Thessalonians 5:20
Summary
God hides greatness in small packages, whatever God is doing in your life may look small, slow, or insignificant, but it is lasting.
Conclusion
God is mighty, yet He does not despise anyone! He is mighty in both power and understanding, for the Lord heareth the poor and despiseth not his prisoners.
Prayer Points
  • Father, help me never to despise small beginnings, tasks, or opportunities.
  • Father, remove from me every spirit of pride and arrogance.
  • Lord, help me to show love and kindness to all, never despising anyone.
  • Father, teach me to trust your timing and not despise small steps in my journey to greatness.
  • O God, I receive the grace to be diligent and faithful to the end.
