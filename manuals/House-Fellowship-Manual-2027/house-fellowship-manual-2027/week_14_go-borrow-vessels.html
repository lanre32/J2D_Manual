RCCG House Fellowship Manual

Week 14 — SUNDAY, 6TH DECEMBER 2026

GO BORROW VESSELS

Opening Prayer
Lord, enlarge my capacity and give me the grace to hearken to Your instructions.
Previous Knowledge
Leaders are to remind the members of the discussion on end of the first quarter.
Memory Verse
“And he saith, Go, borrow thee vessels abroad of all thy neighbours, even empty vessels, borrow not a few.” 2 kings 4:3
Bible Text
2 kings 4:1-7
Introduction
Go borrow vessels is a divine instruction to everyone, vessels that will be used to carry breakthroughs which will take care of needs beyond your reach. God always releases a prophetic command that unlocks abundance. This lesson reveals to us that the answer to our requests lies in obedience, preparation and faith in God.
Lesson Outline One
WHAT DO YOU HAVE?
God works with what you present. The vessels mentioned in this lesson represents capacity and expectation God will only fill what you make available. Limited capacity equals limited results. God cannot pour new oil into a closed or small vessel. What God expects from you as vessel is; a. Your faith. Hebrews 11:6 b. Your mindset and preparation. Luke 1:37, Phillipians 4:13 c. Your skills and opportunity. David rode on the opportunity and skill he had so as to conquer Goliath.
Lesson Outline Two
FOLLOW DIVINE INSTRUCTIONS
Many times, miracles do not come wrapped as miracles, they come as instructions. The size of your obedience determines the size of your manifestation. Go borrow vessels was the instruction and this means; Movement precedes miracles. God will crown your steps with success not your idleness. Having faith without action is failure. God starts with emptiness, not fullness
Summary
When the widow obeyed, the oil flowed until the last vessel was filled. Your miracle is also waiting for your obedience, your capacity and your expectations.
Prayer Points
  • Father, release supernatural provision in my life and household.
  • Father, deliver me from every form of lack and hopelessness.
  • Lord, make doors of opportunities to be open for me and my household.
  • Father, take the little I have and multiply it into abundance to meet my needs and to bless others.
  • O God, give me the grace to obey Your instructions fully even when they seem unusual.
Golden Diet For The Week
Monday Divine instruction may seem strange but follow it.
Tuesday That little in your hand may set the pace to your abundance.
Wednesday Faith without actions does not produce results.
Thursday Obedience leads to great results. Job 36:11
Saturday When you trust God, be expectant to receive special visitation. Proverbs 3:5-6.
