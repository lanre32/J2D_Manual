Week 15 — SUNDAY, 13TH DECEMBER 2026

THE PRINCIPAL THING

Memory Verse
“Wisdom is the principal thing; therefore get wisdom: and with all thy getting get understanding.” Proverbs 4:7
Bible Text
Proverbs 4:1-9
Introduction
It is written, Wisdom is the principal thing, and we are admonished to get wisdom (Proverbs 4:7). Principal means important, consequential or influential. Wisdom is a must have for every believer which is why the Word of God tells us to ask the Lord for wisdom if we lack it (James 1:5). God expects us to place wisdom as a top priority in our desires. It is to be desired more than anything one may need in this world.
Lesson Outline One
WISDOM IS THE PRINCIPAL THING
Wisdom is the most important thing. While we are focusing on the principal thing, it is important to note that the wisdom referred to in our bible text is the wisdom that comes from God and brings life and peace and should not be mistaken for earthly wisdom which comes with envying and strife (James 3:14-16). The wisdom that comes from above possesses the following features: 1. Wisdom comes from God. Proverbs 2:6 2. Wisdom is pure, peaceable, full of mercy and good fruits, without partiality and hypocrisy. James 3:16 3. Wisdom is better than rubies and nothing that one desires can be compared to her. Proverbs 8:11. 4. Wisdom is better than strength. Ecclesiastes 9:16. 5. Wisdom is better than weapons of war. Ecclesiastes 9:18. 6. Wisdom promotes and brings honour to those who embrace her. Proverbs 4:8. 7. Wisdom gives grace and the crown of glory. Proverbs 4:9. 8. Wisdom gives long life, riches and honour. Proverbs 3:16
Lesson Outline Two
WAYS TO ACCESS GOD'S WISDOM
Know the wisdom of God - The Lord Jesus Christ. 1 Corinthians 1:24. Ask God for wisdom. James 1:5 Fear God. Proverbs 1:7 Listen to wisdom. Proverbs 1:33, Proverbs 2:2, Proverbs 8:34 Seek for wisdom as a hidden treasure. Proverbs 2:4-6. Love and seek wisdom early. Proverbs 8:17 Keep God's commandments. Ecclesiastes 12:1
Conclusion
Wisdom is the principal thing. God is the source of wisdom and knowing Jesus Christ gives us access to the wisdom of God. God commands us to ask for wisdom and to listen to His instructions and obey His commandments to retain wisdom.
Prayer Points
