ONE THING IS NEEDFUL

Memory Verse
“But one thing is needful: and Mary hath chosen that good part, which shall not be taken away from her”. Luke 10:42.
Bible Text
Luke 10: 38-42 INTRODUCTION. One thing is needful, that's what Jesus says to Martha in Luke 10:38-42. Five verses that spoke about two sisters with polar opposite of attitudes and actions. From it, we learned about what Jesus desire from us above all things.
Lesson Outline One
PRIORITIZE FELLOWSHIP WITH GOD
Mary was commended by the Lord for her choice to seek His face, sit at His feet and soak up His word. She chose the good part and He was not going to pull her away from that intimate time with Him. Martha had a choice too, but she was focused on the physical service to the Lord rather than on the spiritual sustenance she could receive from Jesus. Mary had a heart to worship and Martha had a mind to work. The most beneficial thing we can do is to worship Jesus. Does that mean we should drop all our other responsibilities and sit around to read the Bible all day? Of course not, the Lord saved is to do good works, evangelize and disciple. (Ephesians 2:20, Matthew, 28:29-20, Titus. 2:3-5) But worship should always come first. Psalm. 73:25 KJV. Whom have I in heaven but thee? And there is none upon Earth that I desire beside thee.
Lesson Outline Two
GAINS OF FELLOWSHIP WITH GOD
Gains of fellowship with God includes: 1. Spiritual growth: Fellowship places you on a pathway for cultivating spiritual growth by learning from each other and growing in your faith. (Heb. 10:25 2. Deeper understanding: It deepens your understanding of God's love and strengthens your relationship with Him.1 John, 4:8 3. Biblical foundation: Fellowship encourages you to look to the word of God for understanding, as opposed to introspection. Heb. 4:12. 4. Unity and love: Fellowship builds unity among believers, encouraging harmony and acceptance despite differences. It provides opportunities for practicing sacrificial love, kindness, forgiveness and serving others. Psalm 131:1-2 Dangers of missing fellowship: Missing fellowship with God can lead to spiritual decline such as: Loss of spiritual support: You miss out support and encouragement from the community of believers which is vital for spiritual growth especially during difficult times. Proverbs 27:17 Spiritual weakness: Without regular spiritual nourishment and correction from the scripture, a person can become spiritually weak and susceptible to sin and negative influences. Heb. 12:6. Increased temptation: As you move away from fellowship with God, you become vulnerable to the "works of darkness'' and ''vain and sinful pressures'' of the world. 1 John. 2:15-17.
Conclusion
And one thing is needful and Mary hath chosen that good part. It is therefore expedient for you and me to choose to serve God in truth and in spirit, in worship, in obedience to His word and in fellowship with the brethren because it is a command. (Hebrews 10:25)
Prayer Points
  • Father, create in me a deep hunger and thirst for Your world.
  • Father, give every believer listening heart that sits at Your feet and hears you audibly.
  • Father, help me to value eternal things above temporary concerns and pleasures.
  • Father, deliver me from unnecessary distractions and anxieties that can draw my heart from You.
  • Father, keep the fire in Daddy and Mummy G.O burning till You come.
