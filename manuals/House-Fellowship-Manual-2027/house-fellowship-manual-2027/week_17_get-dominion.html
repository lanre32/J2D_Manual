Week 17 — SUNDAY, 27TH DECEMBER, 2026

GET DOMINION

Memory Verse
“...and have dominion over the fish of the sea, and over the fowl of the air, and over every living thing that moveth upon the earth”. Genesis 1:28.
Bible Text
Joshua 1:3-8 INTRODUCTION. Dominion is God's delegated authority to all His children redeemed by the blood of His Son, Jesus'. It is an authority given to believers to represent Almighty God on earth with the mandate to execute divine will and establish God's counsel to mankind. Man was made by God to rule, subdue and overcome; therefore, every child of the Most High God has the DNA of dominion.
Lesson Outline One
DOMINION
Dominion originated from the word DOMAIN, an area where authority is exercised, a field or realm of operation, a territory someone rules. The world is our domain and as God's representative we are commissioned with divine authority to rule and reign. In Revelation 5:10, bible says God has made us believers priest and king to reign on earth. Dominion is not automatic; it must be activated by declaring it and walking in righteousness. Dominion requires waking up to responsibility. In Genesis God gave man the responsibility to tend, to guard and manage. In Genesis 2:15, Dominion is enforced through spiritual understanding. In Hosea 4:6, God expects His children to take responsibility for your domain by exercising godly influence and effect your ruling in that space. You are made to be masters over everything God made; that is what you are made for.
Lesson Outline Two
GET DOMINION
In Luke 10:19, bible says ''Behold, I have give you power to tread on serpents and scorpions and over the power of the enemy, nothing shall by any means injure you'' Dominion is exercised through; a. The Word of God. b. Faith in God and His Word. Hebrews 11:6 c. Obedience. Job 36:11 d. Diligence and flawless commitment. 1 Samuel 17, Proverbs 22:29. e. Prayer. Luke 18:1 Dominion is rooted in having identity as God's children. When you understand your identity in God: Life of it Cannot Be Done disappears. Philippians 4:13. Inferiorty complex vanishes. Just like Joshua and Caleb declared possibility in Numbers 14 Authority becomes natural and daily manifestation. David brought down Goliath. Fear loses its grip.
Conclusion
God has not called us to be dominated by fear, sin, sickness, and poverty or darkness (act of the devil). In Christ, you are restored, authorized and empowered therefore stand in your God - given dominion and reign in life.
Prayer Points
  • Father, I receive the grace to walk in dominion You have ordained for me.
  • Father, subdue every power contending with my God -given dominion.
  • Father, open my eyes to the knowledge of Your word that establishes dominion and authority.
  • Father, grant me dominion over sin, the flesh and every weakness that seeks to rule my life.
  • Father, let Your fire consume every chain hindering me from exercising my dominion.
