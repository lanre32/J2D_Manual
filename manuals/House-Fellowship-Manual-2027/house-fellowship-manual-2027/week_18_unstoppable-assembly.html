Week 18 — THANKSGIVING SUNDAY, 3RD JANUARY 2027

UNSTOPPABLE ASSEMBLY

Memory Verse
“And I say also unto thee, That thou art Peter, and upon this rock I will build My church; and the gates of hell shall not prevail against it”. Matthew 16:18.
Bible Text
John 16:332
Introduction
In Leviticus 26:7, bible says the unstoppable assembly shall chase their enemies and their enemies shall fall by the sword before them; an unstoppable assembly is not defined by numbers, 2 Chronicles 20:1-20, weapons or human strength but by having divine backing, unity, unalloyed dedication and a sense of purpose to the call or assignment. They are God-led. In Exodus 13:21 bible says God went before Israel as a pillar of fire and cloud. Their victory was guaranteed because they carried divine presence and they move with God.
Lesson Outline One
THERE IS A PROMISE
An unstoppable assembly has the promise of God's presence, Hebrews 13:5, God's interventions, Jeremiah 32:27, divine deliverance from the lion's den or fiery furnace. This army makes wonders through their faith in God's ability, they are more than conquerors, Romans 8:37, and they can subdue nations and kingdoms, Hebrews 11:33-34. They carry a spiritual fire and an heavenly authority, in Joel 2:3 bible says fire devours before them, nothing escapes, and they can tread on scorpions, serpents and will never be hurt. Luke 10:19 But they are a disciplined people. In 2 Timothy 2:3-4, bible says a good soldier avoids entanglement, Discipline keeps the unstoppable assembly strong and focused.
Lesson Outline Two
UNSTOPPABLE ASSEMBLY IS EMPOWERED
In Zechariah 4:6, bible says it is not by might nor by power, but by My Spirit says the Lord of hosts. Every assembly may be physically prepared but only the empowered assembly is unstoppable. God does not send His people to battle empty handed, He empowers them. What are they empowered with? With His Spirit. Acts 10:38. With His presence. Hebrews 13:5 With His authority. Luke 10:19. With His strength. Philippians 4:13. When they are empowered, they become fearless, Daniel 3, obedient, Isaiah 1:19, they produce great results, 2 kings 4:1-7, they restore perfect order, Luke 7:11-15, they exercise their authority and flee, run away from all appearances of evil. 1 Thessalonians5:22, 1 Timothy 6:11, 2 Timothy 2:22.
Conclusion
God's unstoppable assembly is not defined by size but by divine empowerment. When the Spirit of God rests upon His assembly, barriers crumble, enemies scatter, fiery furnaces become air conditioned, and territories submit
Prayer Points
  • Father, enlist me in your end time army and make me a vessel fit for your use.
  • Father, clothe your children with power, boldness and fire of the Holy Ghost.
  • Father, let every opposition standing against Your army be scattered.
  • Father, strengthen your children to stand firm to the end without compromise.
  • Father, let Your fire detach everything that negates fulfilment of Your children's assignment.
Golden Diet For The Week
Monday Through the name of Jesus, an unstoppable assembly works wonders. Psalm 44:5
Tuesday In all these things we face in life, we are more than conquerors. Romans 8:37
Wednesday God wroughts great miracles through His anointed people.
Thursday To succeed in a divine assignment, you need to put on the whole armour of God. Ephesians 6:11-1
Saturday Gates of hell cannot prevail against His church. Matthew 16:18
Sunday God knows how to handle adversaries and their wicked plots. 2 Chronicles 20:1-20
