Week 19 — SUNDAY, 10TH JANUARY 2027

GAME CHANGERS

Memory Verse
“And when they found them not, they drew Jason and certain brethren unto the rulers of the city, crying, These that have turned the world upside down are come hither also; Acts 17:6.
Bible Text
Acts 4:29-33 INTRODUCTION. In every life, there is a moment when everything changes, goals, careers, decisions that shift the outcome of things. In life and destiny, God specializes in game changing moments. When God comes into an awkward situation, the rule changes, the direction and end result will also change. Game changer can turn night to morning, sorrow to joy and sickness to wholeness.
Lesson Outline One
THE ALMIGHTY IS THE GAME CHANGER
In every situation of life, there comes a moment when human strength ends. That moment is where the Almighty God steps in as the Game Changer. God can overrule demonic decisions, laws and verdicts. 2 Chronicles 20. God can break in for His children to breakthrough. Exodus 14. The Almighty God can turn frustration to celebration. Luke 5. Peter's toiling turned great abundance. When God steps in prison doors can open without humans operating them. Acts 16:25-26. God has the capacity to change times and seasons. Daniel 2:21. He has the ability to change stories and identity. Genesis 30:22. Luke 1:37, Acts 10. God changed the identities of Rachel and Zechariah's and Cornelius.
Lesson Outline Two
ATTRIBUTES OF GOD INSPIRED GAME CHANGERS
Let's us look at the special attributes of God inspired game changers. They pray in the face of trials and temptations, they turn challenges to testimonies. Daniel 6:20-23 They are bold and trigger divine responses. Daniel 3:16-26 They are known for exceptional power and commands supernatural signs. Acts 4:30 They experience and enjoy divine visitation. Acts 12:7-11 They are filled again and again with the Holy Ghost. Acts 4:31. They operate in great grace. Acts 4:33
Conclusion
God is the known Game Changer and He has endowed His children to continue too, without fear, with power that produces destiny- shifting testimonies.
Prayer Points
  • Father, arise and change every negative pattern in my life.
  • Every power assigned to keep me stagnant be overturned In Jesus' Name.
  • Father, empower me to become game changer in Jesus' Name.
  • Father, announce Your glory in my life.
  • O God of turnaround, please visit me this season in Jesus' Name.
