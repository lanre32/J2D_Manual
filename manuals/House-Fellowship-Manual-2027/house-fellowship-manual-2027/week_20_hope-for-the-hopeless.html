Week 20 — SUNDAY, 17TH JANUARY 2027

HOPE FOR THE HOPELESS

Memory Verse
“And when the Lord saw her, He had compassion on her, and said unto her, Weep not”. Luke 7:13
Bible Text
Luke 7:11-15 INTRODUCTION. HOPELESS means a state where a person feels that there is no possibility of improvement, help, or positive change. It is the feeling that a situation is beyond solution or recovery. To be hopeless is to believe that nothing good will happen again, efforts are useless, Luke 5:1-7, future holds no promise but when hope is enacted the future promises will be established and efforts will be compensated with fruitful results.
Lesson Outline One
GOD KNOWS THE HOPELESS
In our bible text, there was a story of the widow of Nain about to bury her only child when Jesus stepped in. Luke 7:11-15. What does God see in that hopeless situation? He sees success in frustrating challenges. Luke 5:1-7 He sees life in dead situation. Luke 7:11-17, Mark 5 He sees freedom in that prison of life. Acts 16:25-26. He sees Goliath and Haman being subdued and conquered. He sees the recovery of losses. Joel 2:25-28. He sees children and not dead wombs. Genesis 30:22 Hopeless situations are never hidden from God, He sees and acts, you may feel forgotten but you are never forsaken.
Lesson Outline Two
HOW CAN ONE REGAIN OR OBTAIN HOPE?
Hope can be restored or regained through: The mercy of God. Lamentations 3:22-23, bible says it is of the Lord's mercies we are not consumed. Your seed of commitment in God's service. Prayer and ceaseless supplication. Rugged faith. Mark 5:25-34. Faith connects God's children to God's power. Divine intervention. Luke 7:11-15. Jesus stopped the funeral and restored life. Declaration of God's Word. Philippians 4:13, Psalm 107:20. Hope grows when we believe God's Word. God's act of kindness. John 9:1-5. A Life of joy and peace. EXAMPLES OF HOPELESS SITUATIONS THAT RECEIVED HOPE. Hannah - Barrenness turned to Joy. 1 Samuel 1:2, 10-20. Joseph - From Prison to Palace. Genesis 39-41 Widow of Zarephath - Last Meal turned to overflow. 1 kings 17:8-16 The woman with the issue of blood - Chronic sickness was healed. Mark 5:25-34 Blind Bartimaues - Blindness to Vision. Mark 10:46-52
Conclusion
If God is present, then your story is not over.
Prayer Points
  • Father, breathe life into every dead situation in my life.
  • Father, by Your mercy turn my tears to testimonies.
  • Father, visit me with divine encouragement in my lowest moment.
  • Father, grant me the grace to hope again and rise again.
  • Father, let hope arise where help seems absent.
