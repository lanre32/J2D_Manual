Week 21 — SUNDAY, 24TH JANUARY, 2027

MISUSED OPPORTUNITY

Memory Verse
“But Gehazi the servant of Elisha the man of God said, behold my master hath spared Naaman this Syrian in not receiving at his hands that which he brought: but as the Lord liveth, I will run after him, and take somewhat of him”. 2 Kings 5:20
Bible Text
2 Kings 5:1-27 INTRODUCTION. Opportunities are divine privileges. When God places His children near power, servants, and His work, He is giving them chance to grow in character and destiny had a rare opportunity - to serve a prophet, learn obedience, and inherit greatness - but he misused that opportunity and paid a heavy price. Opportunity can lift you or ruin you, depending on you handle it.
Lesson Outline One
OPPORTUNITY GIVES ACCESS
Gehazi had opportunity to work with great prophet of Most High(Elijah), witnessed great miracles and made kings. Many prayed for such access but Gehazi treated it casually, in Matthew 22:14, bible says many are called but few are chosen. BENEFITS OF USING OPPORTUNITY GIVEN It leads to destiny fulfilment. David became king through opportunity of defeating Goliath. It builds character and maturity. Luke 16:10. It attracts divine promotion and prevents future regret. James 4:10 It releases God's favour. Ruth gained favour from Boaz. BIBLICAL EXAMPLES OF THOSE THAT UTILISED THEIR OPPORTUNITY. David - destroyed Goliath through the opportunity. 1 Samuel 17. Mordecai and Esther - filled the vacancy in the palace of king Ahasuerus. Esther 4:14 Caleb - got Hebron by defending God's mandate. Numbers 14: Abraham - Got a new name through obedience to God's instruction. Daniel and three Hebrew boys. Daniel 3 & 6
Lesson Outline Two
THE PRICE OF MISUSED OPPORTUNITY
Gehazi gained Naaman's curse through covetousness, “the leprosy shall cleave unto thee and unto thy seed forever” 2kings 5:27. What you gained dishonestly will eventually destroy what you value most. What is the price for the misused opportunities? Loss of position. King Saul lost the throne to David Loss of future. Samson lost glorious future to Delilah's lap. Loss of legacy. Eli lost the legacy and God's mandate to indifference to his children bad behavior. Loss of double portion of Elisha's anointing. Life ended in shame Loss of destiny. Lot lost his children destinies to lack of caution to in choosing Sodom and Gomorroh. Loss of continuous victory over adversaries.
Conclusion
Gehazi teaches that opportunity is a test. What you do when no one is watching determines whether your opportunity becomes a blessing or a curse. Do not misuse opportunity God has given you.
Prayer Points
  • Father, restore every good opportunity I have misused knowingly or unknowingly.
  • I receive grace to recognize divine visitation when it comes.
  • Lord, don't let mistakes define my destiny but your mercy.
  • Father where other failed, let me succeed by divine wisdom.
  • I reject laziness and procrastination In Jesus Name.
