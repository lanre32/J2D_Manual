Week 22 — SUNDAY, 31ST JANUARY 2027

OVERCOMING SELF

Memory Verse
“And he said to them all, If any man will come after Me, let him deny himself, and take up his cross daily, and follow Me”. Luke 9:23
Bible Text
Romans 12:1-2 INTRODUCTION. One of the greatest battles a believer fights is not against the devil, people or challenging issues, but against self. Self wants comfort like Jonah inside the raging storm, recognition like Absalom, and control like Korah, Dathan and Abiram and independence like the prodigal son. Until self is conquered, destiny will suffer and testimonies will be hindered.
Lesson Outline One
WHAT IS SELF?
Self represents: Lust of the eyes, of the flesh and the pride of life. 1 John 2:15 Personal will over God's will. Ingratitude. Samson suffered from ingratitude to God. Lack of commitment to divine purpose. Being indifferent to divine counsel. Eli was indifferent and he suffered a terrible ending. BIBLICAL EXAMPLES OF LIFE DOMINATED WITH SELF. Adam - Self destroyed dominion. Genesis 3:6 Saul - Self ruined his calling to kingship. 1 Samuel 15:24 Absalom - Self destroyed God's plan for him. Lot - Self allowed him to make the wrong choice of staying in Sodom and Gomorrah. Demas - loved things of this world and forsook God.
Lesson Outline Two
HOW TO OVERCOME SELF?
Let us look at how to address self and overcome it. Being renewed daily by the word of God. John 17:17, Psalm 119:9,11 Daily walking in the Spirit. Galatians 5:16. Choose humility. James 4:6 Renounce gathering of self. Prioritize companies. 1 Corinthians 15:33 Crucify the flesh embracing the cross. Galatians 2:20
Summary
When you overcome self, you will enjoy freedom from pride, deeper intimacy with God, greater spiritual authority and fulfilment of destiny.
Conclusion
You cannot follow Christ and follow self at the same time. The throne of your heart can only have one ruler. When self steps down, Christ steps up.
Prayer Points
  • Father, deliver me from the power of self that wars against Your will in my life.
  • Holy Spirit, subdue my flesh and strengthen my inner man.
  • Father, help me to rule my spirit and not be ruled by emotions.
  • Every pride and self-exaltation blocking my spiritual growth, be uprooted.
  • Father, give me the grace to deny myself and follow You daily.
