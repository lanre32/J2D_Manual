Week 23 — THANKSGIVING SUNDAY, 7TH FEBRUARY 2027

OVERCOMING THE WORLD

Memory Verse
“For whatsoever is born of God overcometh the world: and this the victory that overcometh the world, even our faith”. 1 John5:4
Bible Text
1 John 5:4-5 INTRODUCTION. Believers may be living in this world but they are not to live based on the worldly system, though the world system may pressure us to sin, fear, pride, lust, unbelief but as Christians we ought to take our stand in faith and purity of hearts. God never called us to be defeated by the world; He called us to overcome it. Overcoming the world and its system is not by human strength but by divine life and faith in Christ.
Lesson Outline One
JESUS OVERCAME THE WORLD
In John 16:33, bible says in the world ye shall have tribulation: but be of good cheer, I have overcome the world. Jesus paid the price of redemption for mankind by overcoming: Sin and its wages. Romans 3:23 Your adversary the Devil (1 Peter 5:8.) The flesh. 1 John 2:15-17 The victory received through the price Jesus paid made believers overcomers by; Gaining access to eternal life. Revelations 12:11 Having authority with Christ. Receiving crowns and glory.
Lesson Outline Two
WHO ARE THE OVERCOMERS?
Those that walk in the Spirit. Those that carry the life of God. Those that believe in the word of God. Those that trust in God, depend on Christ. Those that have confidence in God's promises. HOW TO OVERCOME THE WORLD? By the Word of God, the word renews the mind and strengthens the Spirit. Psalm 119:11. -By the Spirit of God. Galatians 5:16. The Spirit empowers holy living. By separation and discipline. 2 Corinthians 6:17. You cannot overcome what you continually embrace.
Conclusion
The world may challenge you, but it cannot defeat you if you are in Christ. You are not fighting for victory, you are fighting from victory.
Prayer Points
  • Father, empower me by the Holy Spirit to live above the pressures and temptations of this world.
  • I break free from every worldly attachment pulling my heart away from God.
  • Father, help me to love righteousness and hate iniquity in all its forms.
  • Lord, silence every voice of the world speaking defeat into my destiny.
  • Father, renew my mind daily to align with God's truth and not worldly deception.
