Week 24 — SUNDAY, 14TH FEBRUARY, 2027

CHRISTIANS AND POLITICS PART 1

Memory Verse
“When the righteous is in authority, the people rejoice: but when the wicked beareth rule, the people mourn”. Proverbs 29:2.
Bible Text
Genesis 41:38-41 INTRODUCTION. Politics refers to the activities associated with the governance of a country or an area. Psalm 24:1 tells us that the earth belongs to our God and all the people that live in the world because He is the Maker, Deuteronomy 10:14. As the Owner and Maker of the earth, God remains in charge of the governance of every nation and kingdom (Psalm 47:2) but gives authority to men at different times to be in governance taking instructions from Him as the King of Kings. Daniel 2:21
Lesson Outline One
IN THE BEGINNING
The bible records that in the beginning God made the heavens and earth (Genesis 1:1), made Adam and gave him dominion to rule over all His creation. Genesis 1:28. And from Adam, God governed the kingdoms of the earth through His selected and appointed sons and daughters: Judges like Deborah, Gideon, and Samson were raised up by God as leaders in response to specific needs. Judges 2:16. At various times God demonstrated His sovereignty over the whole earth by placing His sons and daughters in positions of power across the nations of the earth: Joseph rose to political power in Egypt through God's interference in the affairs of that nation to save His people from famine. Genesis 41. Daniel, a Jew, served in the courts of Babylon and Persia. Daniel 1-6. Esther became a queen in Shushan and saved her people from genocide. Esther 4
Lesson Outline Two
THE PEOPLE'S CHOICE
The children of Israel demanded for a king to be like other nations (1 Samuel 8:4-5) and God told Prophet Samuel to anoint Saul as king over Israel, 1 Samuel 8:22. That was the people's choices. However, because man's ways are not God's ways, man's ways are corrupt and wicked. Jeremiah 17:9, people's choice never result in good outcomes, 1 Samuel 8:11-21. All the admonitions of the Prophet Samuel came to pass when Saul became their king. He took over the land and the whole nation became his subjects which is what many nations are experiencing today because they refuse to allow God to choose their leaders so that He can rule through them. We are admonished to always allow God to be in charge of the governance of our communities, cities, states, and nations for us to enjoy God's divine plan for peace and prosperity. Jeremiah 29:11, Proverbs 23:12-13
Summary
God is the Owner and Ruler of the earth (Psalm 24:1) and has given His sons and daughters the privileges to run the affairs of the governance (Psalm 47:2). We must allow Him to choose our leaders so that He can rule through them. 1 kings 3:7-14.
Conclusion
The plan of God for every nation of the world is to enjoy peace and live in prosperity (3 John vs2), under His divine governance. Jeremiah 32: 38-40. When the righteous are in authority, the people rejoice because God will be governing them.
Prayer Points
  • Father, we enthrone You as the King over nations.
  • Father, please take charge of the governance of our nation.
  • Father, please uproot every leader, whether male or female in authority that You have not appointed.
  • Father, help us to obey You in the appointment of our leaders.
  • Father, please let there be peace and prosperity in our nation.
