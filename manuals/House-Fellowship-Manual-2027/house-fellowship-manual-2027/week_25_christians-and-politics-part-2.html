Week 25 — SUNDAY, 21ST FEBRUARY 2027

CHRISTIANS AND POLITICS PART 2

Memory Verse
“When the righteous are in authority, the people rejoice: but when the wicked beareth rule, the people mourn”. Proverbs 29:2.
Bible Text
Daniel 6:3-5 INTRODUCTION. Politics refers to the activities associated with the governance of a country or area. Psalm 24:1 tells us that the earth belongs to our God and all the people that live in the world because He is the Maker, Deuteronomy 10:14. As the Owner and Maker of the earth, God remains in charge of the governance of every nation and kingdom (Psalm 47:2) but gives authority to men at different times to be in governance taking instructions from Him as the King of Kings. Daniel 2:21. We will be studying the biblical guideline for God's children in politics.
Lesson Outline One
WHY CHRISTIANS SHOULD PARTICIPATE IN POLITICS?
Politics refers to the activities associated with the governance of a country or an area and these activities affect nearly every part of dailylife: the laws we obey, the rights we enjoy, the freedoms we cherish are determined by the leaders that govern. Proverbs 29:2, 1 Timothy 2:1-2. Matthew 5:13-14 tells us that we are the salt of the earth which means that as Christians, we are to influence every sphere of society, including politics, with integrity, truth, grace and with our prayers. Jeremiah 29:7. At creation, God handed over the governance of the earth through His selected and appointed sons and daughters like Deborah, Gideon and Samson, Saul, David, Solomon. e.t.c. As Christians, like king Solomon we are to take charge of the governance of our villages, cities, towns, states and countries for our Lord so that the people can rejoice and always give thanks to God for all His goodness. 1 Kings 4:21-25, our God is the Governor of all nations and is interested in rulership at all nation, Psalm 22:28 and He has handed over the governance to His sons and daughter of whom we are. Psalm 115:16
Lesson Outline Two
THE GUIDELINES
As Christians we should get involved in politics with the banner of Jesus lifted up as the ambassadors of our Lord Jesus Christ, 2 Corinthians 5:20. As ambassadors for Christ, we should be shining examples for the Lord in politics and governance John 5:39; some of the guideline we should follow in our involvement: · Let your light shine. Matthew 5:13-16. Christians are to influence society, including politics with integrity and truth. The light of God shone through Daniel 6:1-3. Justice, mercy and humanity. Micah 6:8. Daniel humbled himself before the Lord. Daniel 9:3. Faithfulness to God and accountability. 1 Timothy 4:12, 1 Corinthians 4:1-2. Obedience to God above man. Acts 4:19-20. Remember that you will give account of your stewardship. Colossians 3:2-24, Galatians 6:9, 2 Corin. 5:10
Summary
As Christians our involvement in politics should be for good governance that will enhance the wellbeing of the people. When we follow the guidelines set out by God, the Owner of the heavens and the earth, our villages, cities, towns, states and countries will prosper and the people will rejoice and give God the glory.
Conclusion
God is interested in the prosperity of all nations of the world that submit to His authority. Jeremiah 32:38-40.
Prayer Points
  • Father, we enthrone You as the King over the nations.
  • Father, please take charge of the governance of our nation.
  • Father, please uproot every leader, whether male or female in authority that You have not appointed.
  • Father, please help us to shine Your light as Christians in the governance of our nation.
  • Father, please let our leaders lead us according to Your will for our nation.
