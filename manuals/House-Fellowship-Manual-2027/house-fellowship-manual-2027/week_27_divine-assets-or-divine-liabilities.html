Week 27 — THANKSGIVING SUNDAY, 7TH MARCH, 2027

DIVINE ASSETS OR DIVINE LIABILITIES?

Memory Verse
“And the Lord said, Shall I hide from Abraham that thing which I do;” Genesis 18:17
Bible Text
Genesis 18:17-22 INTRODUCTION. In kingdom business people are evaluated as assets or liabilities depending on Your fellowship and closeness to the will of God. The Almighty God also assesses lives whether productive or inactive, God looks at individuals not by their wealth or status, but by their spiritual value, obedience and alignment with God's purpose.
Lesson Outline One
WHERE DO YOU STAND, ASSET OR LIABILITY?
A divine asset is the one that: God trusts with secrets. Psalm 25:14. Has been proven, faithful and walks in intimacy with God like Enoch did . Influences God's action. Genesis 18:22. That prays beyond personal needs and stands in the gap as a watchman. Exodus 22:30 Builds spiritual legacy and thinks beyond now. A Liability is a believer that: 1. No longer advances God's purpose but instead hinders God's work. (John3vs9-10) 2. Lacks spiritual growth still remains a baby. 3. That cannot handle correction and deeper truth. 4. Disobeys God's word, knows the truth but refuses to act. 5. Is worldly in nature, compromises values and fears people more than God.
Lesson Outline Two
ASSETS ARE PECULIAR
Assets are peculiar because: They have the burden for lost souls. Matthew 9:37. They are kingdom-minded not activities or church centered. They sacrifice to grow God's kingdom but not blessing oriented. Luke 18:18. They obey spiritual leadership, cherishes accountability and follow order. They are faithful in small commitment. Luke 16:10. They are cautious in speech, humble and not arrogant.
Conclusion
Heaven is still asking, ''is this person an asset or a liability ''to My agenda? God is not looking for perfection, but for availability, alignment, and intercession.
Prayer Points
  • Father, thank You for counting me worthy to be used in Your divine agenda.
  • Father, let my life bring advantage to Your kingdom and not shame.
  • Holy Spirit align my will with divine purpose daily.
  • Every damaging decision I have made be corrected by divine mercy.
  • Lord, let my presence everywhere I go attract favour, progress and victory.
Golden Diet For The Week
Monday When you align with a divine purpose, you remain divine asset. Genesis 18:17-19
Tuesday When you cannot control flesh and its affection, you become a liability to the body of Christ.
Wednesday Greed can turn an asset to a liability. 2 kings 5:20-27.
Thursday Obedience makes one a credible asset. Deuteronomy 28:1-2.
Saturday God will always remove liabilities. Esther 7:10, John 15:2.
Sunday Your inner prompting to divine leading preserves You from being a liability.
