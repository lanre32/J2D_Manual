Week 28 — SUNDAY, 14TH MARCH, 2027

THE PURPOSE MUST NOT BE CHANGED

Memory Verse
“...and with the signet of his lords; that the purpose might not be changed concerning Daniel”. Daniel 6:17
Bible Text
Daniel 6:15-22 INTRODUCTION. Every life has a divine purpose, some purposes are attacked, delayed, or challenged - but they must not be changed. Jealous men plotted against Daniel and manipulated the king into signing a decree but later the counsel of God prevailed.
Lesson Outline One
GOD'S PURPOSE IS STRONGER THAN HUMAN CONSPIRACY
In Daniel 6:4, bible says they could find no charge or fault against Daniel. Enemies tried to rewrite Daniel's destiny but their conspiracy could not cancel divine purpose. Proverbs 19:21. Though God may allow human conspiracy to cause: 1. A Temporary closing of doors. Acts 16:26 2. A brief reign of terror by the enemy. Acts 12. 3. Withdrawal of godly right. 4. Banishment to a lion's den. But God's purpose will be preserved and produce testimony that nations will hear and glorify the name of God.
Lesson Outline Two
GOD DEFENDS PURPOSE
Purpose attracts opposition, the moment a destiny begins to manifest, resistance arises. But this the assurance of scripture: God does not abandon what He ordains, He defends. Every child of God must know that: Purpose is God's idea, not man's invention. Isaiah 46:10. Opposition is proof of purpose. Acts 9:4 God allows the test but controls the outcome. Lamentation 3:37. God defends purpose supernaturally. Daniel 6:22. God defends purpose through integrity. Psalm 84:11. God turns attacks into announcements. Romans 8:28.
Conclusion
If God gave the vision, spoke the word, assigned the calling, God will defended the purpose.
Prayer Points
  • Father, I thank You because your purpose for my life is unchangeable.
  • Father, shatter every conspiracy, decree raised against me.
  • Father, let all traps set up to disqualify me from honour be consumed by fire.
  • I decree that delay, denials and evil intentions will not stop me from shining.
  • Father, overturn every system designed to silence my purpose.
Golden Diet For The Week
Monday God will always defend His purpose, His counsel must stand. Psalm 33:11
Tuesday The purpose of God for His children will survive every opposition. Genesis 50:20.
Wednesday Enemy may seal your Daniel in lions' den but Daniel cannot be stopped. Daniel 6:16-22.
