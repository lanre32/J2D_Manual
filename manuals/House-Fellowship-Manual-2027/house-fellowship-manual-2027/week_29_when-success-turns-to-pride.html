Week 29 — SUNDAY, 21ST MARCH, 2027

WHEN SUCCESS TURNS TO PRIDE

Memory Verse
“But when he was strong, his heart was lifted up to his destruction: for he transgressed against the LORD his God, and went into the temple of the Lord to burn incense upon the altar of incense”. 2 Chr. 26:16
Bible Text
2 Chronicles 26:1-23 INTRODUCTION. King Uzziah is one of the most striking examples in scripture of a man who began well, rose greatly, but ended poorly. His life teaches us that divine success must be handled with humility or it can become a trap.
Lesson Outline One
GODLY BEGINNING
Looking at the glorious beginning of King Uzziah He sought the Lord. 2 Chronicles 26: 3-5. He was spiritually mentored. He experienced victories over enemies. 2 Chronicles 26:6-15. He had fortified cities and strong defenses. He was marvelously helped by God. He had innovation and technological advancement.
Lesson Outline Two
PRIDE OF HEART
In 2 Chronicles 26:16, bible says ''But when he was strong, his heart was lifted up to his destruction''. He entered wrong and destructive non-commissioned office not meant for Him. He crossed the boundaries God never assigned to him. He rejected corrections. 2 Chronicles 26:17-18. He brought leprosy to himself through pride. 2 Chronicles 26:19-21. He was isolated for the rest of his life, his pride brought separation. A glorious beginning does not guarantee a glorious ending. King Uzziah died a leper, he was buried separately from other glorious kings.
Summary
Success is sustained by humility, gratitude, obedience and continual dependence on God. When humility is preserved, success is established but when pride takes over success fades away.
Conclusion
A Lasting success requires a humble heart that constantly acknowledges God as the source and purpose of all achievements.
Prayer Points
  • Father, convert every breakthrough designed to destroy me to glory.
  • Father, please help me never to forget You as my true source of success.
  • Father, please give me wisdom to handle fame, success and honour.
  • Father, keep my heart teachable even when I am celebrated.
  • Lord, deliver me from pride and the spirit of self-exaltation.

Tuesday God resists the proud but gives grace to the humble. 1 Peter 5:5-6
Wednesday Let anyone that thinks he is standing take heed lest he falls. 1 Corinthians 10:12
Thursday When power comes, pride may follow an un- unregenerated heart. 2 Chronicles 26:16
Saturday Covetousness also can set traps for destruction.
Sunday There is grace given by God to be humble even in victory and success.
