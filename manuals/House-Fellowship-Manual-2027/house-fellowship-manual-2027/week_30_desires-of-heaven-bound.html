Week 30 — SUNDAY, 28TH MARCH 2027

DESIRES OF HEAVEN BOUND

Memory Verse
“Set your affection on things above, not on things on the earth”. Colossians 3:2
Bible Text
Colossians 3:1-16 INTRODUCTION Heaven bound means destined for heaven, it describes a person or soul on a spiritual journey towards a celestial afterlife, it implies being saved, born again and living righteously to reach eternal life. It's a life lived with eternity in mind.
Lesson Outline One
DESIRE OF HEAVEN BOUND
Heaven bound Christians should have the following desires: Presence of Christ: A deep craving for Jesus's presence and power. Holiness & Purity: Seeking a pure heart and a holy, blameless life (Matthew 5:8, Hebrews 12:14). Spiritual Transformation: A desire to be a new creature, changed by the Holy Spirit, leaving worldly things behind (2 Corinthians 5:17). Following Jesus: Walking in His footsteps, taking up their cross, and denying self (Mark 8:34). Eternal Perspective: Living with eternity in view, focusing on God's kingdom rather than temporary earthly gains (Hebrew 11:9-10). Love & Service: Working in love and being devoted to kingdom service, helping others. They are in expectation of the second coming of our Lord Jesus Christ. I John 3:3. They look forward to their heavenly reward. 2 Timothy 4:7-8
Lesson Outline Two
GOD'S EXPECTATIONS FOR THE HEAVEN-BOUND
Be born again. (John 3:3). Show fruits of repentance. (2 Corinthians 5:17). Live with the consciousness of eternity. (John 14:1-2). Study the word of God. (2 Timothy 2:15). Be part of kingdom service. Be prayerful. (Luke 18:1). Fellowship with believers and keep good company. Hebrew 10:25
Conclusion
A heaven-bound person desires transformation; they trade earthly temptations for heavenly promises, seeking closeness with God and living a life that reflects His will, marked by faith, holiness, and eternal purpose.
Prayer Points
  • Father, plant in my heart a deep hunger for You above all earthly things.
  • Father, help me to set my affection on things above not to be distracted by this world.
  • Father, cleanse my heart and keep me pure that I may see You.
  • Father, give me the grace to endure present trials with my eyes fixed on eternal glory.
  • Father, help me to see life through the lens of eternity and not temporary gains.
