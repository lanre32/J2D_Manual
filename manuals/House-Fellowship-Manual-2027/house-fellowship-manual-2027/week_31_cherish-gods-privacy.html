Week 31 — THANKSGIVING SUNDAY 4TH APRIL, 2027

CHERISH GOD'S PRIVACY

Memory Verse
“The secret of the LORD is with them that fear Him; and He will shew them His covenant”. Psalm 25:14
Bible Text
Deuteronomy 29:29 INTRODUCTION. In a world where everyone wants instant access to information, God values discretion and intimacy, God will reveal deeper purposes only to the humble and the reverent. The prerequisite of having deeper intimacy with God is in having godly fear.
Lesson Outline One
HOW TO CHERISH GOD'S PRIVACY?
God reveals His covenant progressively, but many are not privileged to understand God's plans and programs. Let us look at how one can cherish God's privacy. By keeping your spiritual experience personal, never flaunt your miracles. Avoid seeking human validation or applause. Obey first and speak later. Let actions reflect God's revelation not words. Meditate and pray privately, build intimacy in secret places. Matthew 6:6 Fear and honour God consistently.
Lesson Outline Two
BENEFITS OF CHERISHING GOD'S PRIVACY AND THE CONSEQUENCES OF IGNORING IT
Let us look at the benefits to enjoy when having fellowship with Almighty God. Showing of God's plans and covenant through divine visitation. Protection from misuse, secrets kept in reverence prevent pride, gossip and misuse. There will be spiritual growth, just like Samuel in 1 Samuel 3, he later led the Israelites for years. God entrusts more to those who handle His secrets responsibly. Consequences of ignoring God's privacy Revealing God's secrets for personal glory leads to pride and destruction. Loss of trust. It may lead to withdrawal of deeper revelation. There will be exposure to attacks, it can bring spiritual harm.
Conclusion
Examine your life, are you always in a rush to reveal top secret God has entrusted to you?
Prayer Points
  • Father, help me to fear You genuinely so I can receive Your secrets.
  • Lord teach me to treasure Your privacy and handle Your revelation with care.
  • Holy Spirit, guide me in obedience and help me honour You in all I share.
  • Father, let Your covenant and purposes be revealed to me in Your perfect time.
  • Lord grant me discernment and wisdom to steward the mysteries of Your kingdom faithfully.
