Week 32 — SUNDAY 11TH APRIL, 2027

MY HOME

Memory Verse
In my Father's house are many mansions: if it were not so, I would have told you. I go to prepare a place for you. John 14: 2
Bible Text
Matthew 6:19-20
Introduction
Everyone desires a home; a home is a place of belonging, safety and ultimate peace. There are two types of homes; one is earthly, which is temporary and the other is heavenly, which is eternal. Our focus today is HEAVEN.
Lesson Outline One
MY HEAVENLY HOME
Heaven is not a fantasy, but real, promised, and prepared by God. John 14:2. Heaven is where we will see God face to face. 1 Corinthians 13:12 Heaven is where sorrow ends and joy begins Rev.21:4 Heaven is full of Celebration, heaven is where there is no sickness nor death. Heaven is a beautiful place made of gold and precious stones. Rev 21:21 Heaven is where there is no darkness. Rev. 22:5.
Lesson Outline Two
ARE YOU PRPARED?
LIVE TODAY FOR ETERNITY Colossians 3:1-2 ("Since then you have been raised with Christ, set your hearts on things above, where Christ is, seated at the right hand of God. Set your minds on things above, not on earthly things.") or 1 Peter 1:3-4 ("Praise be to the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ! In His great mercy, he has given us new birth into a living hope through the resurrection of Jesus Christ from the dead, and into an inheritance that can never perish, spoil or fade. This inheritance is kept in heaven for you. Perspective Shift: Our earthly life is temporary; our heavenly home is eternal. This changes our priorities and values. Endurance and Hope: The hope of heaven empowers us to persevere through trials and difficulties. Holy Living: Knowing where we're headed should motivate us to live lives that honour God now. Sharing the Hope: Our ultimate home is not just for us, but a message of hope to share with a lost world. We are ambassadors. Not Detachment, but Right Attachment: We don't ignore earthly responsibilities, but we hold them loosely, knowing our true citizenship is in heaven (Philippians 3:20).
Conclusion
Your true citizenship is already there (Phil 3:20). So do not fret, when the storms rage, when the bills pile up, when relationships fracture.
Prayer Points
  • Father, thank You for preparing a place for me through Jesus Christ.
  • Lord, set my affection on things above not on earthly things.
  • Father, deliver my heart from distractions that pull me away from heaven.
  • Father, give me grace to live worthy life.
  • Lord, never allow anything to disqualify me from Your kingdom.
