Week 33 — SUNDAY 18TH APRIL, 2027

THE WAITING ROOM

Memory Verse
For the vision is yet for an appointed time, but at the end it shall speak, and not lie: though it tarry, wait for it; because it will surely come, it will not tarry. Habakkuk 2:3 KJV
Bible Text
Job 14:14
Introduction
The most difficult place to be in life is the waiting room: a laboratory, a hospital, an interview, an examination, or waiting on God for a particular miracle. Our focus today is the WAITING ROOM OF GOD: waiting on God may be one of the most difficult aspects of the Christian life. In the book of Acts 1, Jesus commanded His disciples to wait for the promise.
Lesson Outline One
DELAY IS NOT DENIAL
Our God is the God of times and seasons, Eccl. 3. Therefore, there is an appointed time for whatever he wants to do. Zechariah and Elizabeth: Their delay in having a child was orchestrated by God so that their son, John the Baptist, could fulfil the pivotal role of preparing the way for Jesus Christ - the Saviour. Their waiting served a grander redemptive purpose. Abraham and Sarah: Their long wait for the child of promise - Isaac, as described in Genesis 18:10, demonstrated God's faithfulness to His covenant and His ability to act beyond human limitations. The "appointed time" was God's time, not theirs, showcasing His sovereign control over life and destiny. - The Man at the Pool of Bethesda: His 38 years of waiting for healing at the pool (John 5) underscores the theme that even prolonged suffering and delay can be met by God's perfect timing and intervention. Jesus' arrival and direct command brought healing in a way the man could not achieve through his own efforts or the pool's supposed power. - Joseph waited for the fulfilment of His dreams and visions despite all the challenges he faced. God has a purpose for your life. - Jer. 1:5 What you call delay is the season of preparation.
Lesson Outline Two
BENEFITS OF BEING IN GOD'S WAITING ROOM
- Our faith and character are built. James 1:3 - Our strength is being renewed Isaiah 40:31 - Helps us to fulfil purpose. Genesis 21:2, Acts 1. - Helps us to learn to trust God. Psalm 27:14.
Conclusion
God has a set time for everything. If it seems like God is silent or hasn't answered your prayers, it is not because He is sleeping; He is waiting for the appointed time.
Weekly Activity
God never delays without purpose. Explain and cite examples in the bible.
Prayer Points
  • Father, grant me the grace to wait for Your perfect timing.
  • Lord, remove from me every spirit of impatience.
  • Father, help me to achieve purpose as I surrender my plans and timeliness to You.
  • Lord, deliver me from rushing ahead of You.
  • Father, align my life with Your divine calendar.
