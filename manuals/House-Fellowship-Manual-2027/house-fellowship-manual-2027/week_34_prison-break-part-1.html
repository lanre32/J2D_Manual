PRISON BREAK PART 1

Memory Verse
And suddenly there was a great earthquake, so that the foundations of the prison were shaken: and immediately all the doors were opened, and every one's bands were loosed. Acts 16.26.KJV
Bible Text
Acts 16: 23 - 28, John 8:36
Introduction
What comes to mind when you hear the word "Prison"? (Physical confinement, bars, cells). Prison is a place of confinement, where one is kept involuntarily or voluntarily. Prison extends beyond the physical, encompassing spiritual, mental, and emotional bondage, and how our Almighty God is the ultimate Liberator, capable of breaking every chain!
Lesson Outline One
UNDERSTANDING THE DIFFERENT PRISONS
A. The Prison of Addiction: Someone addicted to vices is in prison. The craving, the compulsion, the loss of control; It's an involuntary confinement of the will, trapping individuals in cycles from which they desperately want to escape. It promises escape but delivers deeper bondage. B. The Prison of Demonic Attack/Spiritual Oppression: Someone under demonic attack is in prison. A spiritual battle where forces of darkness seek to bind, torment, and isolate. It can manifest as fear, despair, confusion, or a heavy sense of oppression that feels inescapable. C. Spiritual, Mental, Emotional Prisons: It can be a spiritual, physical, mental or emotional prison. Mental: The prison of anxiety, fear, unforgiveness, negative thought patterns, past trauma that re-plays. Emotional: The prison of bitterness, grief that overwhelms, shame, guilt, a lack of self-worth. Spiritual: Feeling disconnected from God, bound by legalism, doubt, or spiritual apathy. These are often invisible, yet profoundly real and debilitating.
Lesson Outline Two
OUR GREAT DELIVERER
The Almighty God is the God of all impossibilities. While these prisons are real and powerful, we serve a God who is infinitely more powerful. A. He Shakes Foundations and Opens Prison Gates - Paul and Silas - Acts 16:25-26 The physical prison shook, chains fell, doors opened. God can disrupt the very structures that hold us captive - whether they are habits, thought patterns, or spiritual strongholds. He doesn't just unlock the door; sometimes He demolishes the walls of the prison itself.
Conclusion
Are you in the common prison of life? Surrender ALL your challenges to Jesus, the Expert PRISON BREAKER!
Weekly Activity
Mention some events in the bible that talks about prison break.
Prayer Points
  • Father, thank You for every situation I have been through.
  • Lord, pull down prisons, and altars; holding back my destiny fulfilment.
  • Father, destroy every physical or spiritual bondage I am in.
  • Lord, open by fire every door locked against my progress.
  • Father, by the power of the Holy Ghost let the chains holding me down be consumed.
