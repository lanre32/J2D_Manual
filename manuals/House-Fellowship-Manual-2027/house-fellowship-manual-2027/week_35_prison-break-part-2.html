Week 35 — THANKSGIVING SUNDAY, 2ND MAY, 2027

PRISON BREAK PART 2

Memory Verse
If the Son therefore shall make you free, ye shall be free indeed. John 8:36
Bible Text
Luke 8: 26 - 39.
Introduction
What comes to mind when you hear the word "Prison"? (Physical confinement, bars, cells). Prison is a place of confinement, where one is kept involuntarily or voluntarily. Prison extends beyond the physical, encompassing spiritual, mental; And emotional bondage, and how our Almighty God is the Ultimate Liberator, capable of breaking every chain.
Lesson Outline One
STEPS TO FREEDOM
1) Acknowledge your Prison - The first step to freedom is recognizing where you are confined. What invisible chains are holding you? 2) Cry Out to God - Like Paul and Silas, who were praising God in their prison. Like the man at the pool, Jesus sought him out. We must bring our captivity to the One who can deliver. 3) Walk in Your Freedom - Gal.5:1 Freedom is not just an event, but a journey. Once God breaks the chains, we must choose to walk out of the prison. This often involves steps of faith, repentance, seeking help, and renewing our minds. 4) Stay in the place of prayer and the word for sustenance. 4) Be intentional about not going back to the Prison. Keep pressing forward, and your path will shine brighter and brighter every day
Lesson Outline Two
TRUE FREEDOM COMES FROM GOD
Freedom begins with knowing the Truth. John 8:32 Freedom comes by operating in the Holy Spirit's direction. 2 Corinthians 3:17 Freedom is total deliverance from sin's bondage. Romans 6:18. Freedom comes through service. David freed the Israelites from Goliath by offering himself. 1 Samuel 17. It comes from loving God and finding Christ. John 8:36 No prison is too strong for the Almighty God. No chain is too thick for Him to break. Trust in God's liberating power.
Conclusion
If you are in a prison today - spiritual, mental, emotional, or physical - know that God sees you, He hears you, and He is able to set you free. Will you invite Him into your confinement today?
Weekly Activity
What are the dangers of emotional imprisonment? Mention five.
Prayer Points
  • Father, thank You for the penalty you paid for my freedom.
  • Father, I receive the grace to believe you for my freedom.
  • Every lie of the enemy holding me bound be destroyed.
  • Every chain of fear, addiction, delay and limitation is destroyed.
  • Lord, disconnect me from people, places and habits that enslave me.
