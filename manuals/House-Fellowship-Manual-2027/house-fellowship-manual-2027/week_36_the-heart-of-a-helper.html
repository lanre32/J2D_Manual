Week 36 — SUNDAY, 9TH MAY, 2027

THE HEART OF A HELPER

Memory Verse
Beloved, let us love one another: for love is God; and everyone that loveth is born of God and knoweth God. He that loveth not knoweth not God; for God is love. 1 John 4:7-8.
Bible Text
Luke 10:27-37.
Introduction
The heart of a helper is a heart transformed by God's love, compelled by comparison, and committed to action, seeing Christ in every person in need. For us as believers, helping isn't just a good deed; it's a profound expression of our faith. It's not merely an action, but a reflection of a deeper reality within us.
Lesson Outline One
SEEING CHRIST IN OTHERS
Serving the “least of these” Matthew 25:35-40. Jesus makes an astonishing statement: “Whatever you did for one of the least of these brothers and sisters of mine, you did for Me” This elevates every act of help. When we feed the hungry, clothe the naked, visit the sick or the imprisoned, we are ministering directly to Christ Himself. The heart of a helper has eyes to see beyond the outward appearance, beyond social status, beyond perceived worthiness, and to recognize the divine image in every person, especially those in need. Seeing Christ in others help us to break down the barriers of biases, prejudices and fears that might otherwise prevent us from helping others. It reminds us that every person is created in God's image, deeply loved by Him and worthy of our compassion and care. The helper's heart doesn't choose who to help based on their background, but based on their need, seeing Jesus in them.
Lesson Outline Two
HELPING OTHERS
God expects us to share our abundance with the less privileged. 1 John 3:17, Isaiah 1:17. When we don't reach out to others, we are limited, but when we help others, we complement one another in strengths and weaknesses. Eccl. 4:9-10, Romans 16:2. It gives one a sense of purpose and joy within. When you help others; a. It opens doors for divine reward and favour. b. It brings joy and fulfilment to the helper. Acts 20:35. c. It brings unity and compassion in families and communities.
Conclusion
Pray to God to open your eyes to see those who are in need and take a practical step this week to help someone. It doesn't have to be grand; it just needs to be genuine.
Weekly Activity
Discuss Acts 10:27-37 in your centre.
Prayer Points
  • Father, give me the heart that feels the pain of others and responds with love.
  • Father, remove every form of selfishness, pride and indifference from me.
  • Lord, make to be sensitive to divine promptings.
  • Lord, let my actions reveal Your love and bring glory to your name.
  • Lord, raise helpers for me also as I help others.
