Week 37 — SUNDAY, 16TH MAY, 2027

SOCIAL MEDIA

Memory Verse
Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works and glorify your Father which is in heaven. Matthew 5:16
Bible Text
1 Corinthians 10:23
Introduction
Our world has evolved over the years, it has experienced the stone age, the industrial age and now we live in the postindustrial or information age. One key thing that came in this age of information is social media. As Christians, understanding times and seasons and how to live and work with it matters, John 17:14-16, 1 Chronicles 12:32. We need to accept that social media has come to stay, the world has gone beyond that threshold and we are referring to artificial intelligence nowadays. Today we want to know the advantages and disadvantages that are in this innovation so as to thrive even in the midst of turmoil in Jesus name.
Lesson Outline One
ADVANTAGES OF SOCIAL MEDIA
It helps in gaining access to more knowledge than ever. Knowledge is power. John 6:6, 2 Kings 6:13-23. It helps your growth. It makes it easier to connect with people. With the new social apps, you can connect with millions of people from anywhere in the world. It is a tool to propagate the gospel of Christ. There are many people that may never know God unless we use the media to reach them. 1 Corinthians 9:19-23
Lesson Outline Two
DISADVANTAGES OF SOCIAL MEDIA
Although social media is full of advantages for the children of God and for the body of Christ at large, it also could be of disadvantage if not properly harnessed. Distortion of destiny. Dinah's destiny was distorted due to socializing. Genesis 34:1. Corruption of thoughts, actions and decisions. Proverbs 4:23, 1 Corinthians 15:33 It can drive you away from Jesus and who alone is the Way. John 14:6, Proverbs 10:22. It can become addiction, something trying to get God's position in your heart. Joshua 24:23, Exo. 20:3. It devalues harnessing your hidden potentials. §It reduces your time of relating and intimacy with your family.
Conclusion
Social media can be powerful, too; both for good and bad reasons; it all comes down to the use we make of it and the goal we set for ourselves each time we are with our devices. As believers, we must not allow this to become a god in our lives but rather an efficient tool for the propagation of the gospel of Christ.
Weekly Activity
How can believers tame the menace of addiction to social media?
Prayer Points
  • Father, help me to use all social media platforms for the advancement of the gospel.
  • Father, destroy the hold of social media on my life in Jesus' name.
  • Father, help me to locate only the contents that will draw me closer to you.
  • Father, take over the social media space and show Your glory and power through it.
  • Father, use me as an agent of transformation on all platforms I find myself.
Golden Diet For The Week
Monday Guard your heart with all diligence.
Tuesday You can be the change in the midst of filth.
Wednesday Make it a duty for Jesus to be seen through you.
Thursday Trends are ephemeral but God is everlasting.
Friday Change others but don't allow others to change you.
Saturday Plug out the noise and seek to hear from God.
Sunday Live every day with the consciousness of who you are.
