RCCG House Fellowship Manual

Week 38 — SUNDAY, 23RD MAY, 2027

CHILDREN ARE YOUR TOMORROW

Memory Verse
“Train up a child in the way he should go; and when he is old, he will not depart from it. Proverbs 22:6
Bible Text
Genesis 18:17-19
Introduction
A child is expected to be a better version of his or her parents. Your child is supposed to be a new improved you, your child is your future hence losing your child to the devil amounts to losing your life while gaining your child is gaining your future. Never surrender your child to bad company or influence, guide and nurture him/her to become an improved version of you.
Lesson Outline One
FUTURE STARTS WITH SEED
Many parents have destroyed their children's future based on the seed being planted from the cradle. Children are divine investment in the future to be preserved for continuity. Psalm 127:3. Neglecting children means neglecting your tomorrow. What you plant in them, will be the results tomorrow. Train your children in God's pattern in order to secure your legacy. Proverbs 22:6. If you sow good character in your children, you will reap leaders. Watch your children's exposure to screens; what are they seeing? Teach them household chores, godly daily lifestyle, yank out laziness from them.
Lesson Outline Two
HOW TO GUIDE CHILDREN
Children are to be guided, don't leave them alone to themselves, be intentional in their wellbeing. Spiritual foundation ensures children grow with purpose, wisdom and the fear of God. Talent without godliness can destroy a beautiful future. When a generation is not properly taught, darkness will take over. Always lead by example, children learn more from what they see. Teach your children early and consistently. Proverbs 22:6 Combine love with discipline. Allow them to ask questions and express emotions. James 1:19. Guide their spiritual life and encourage your children. Ephesians 6:4. Set boundaries and structure for your children. Proverbs 29:15
Conclusion
Children are not just for today's joy, they are for tomorrow's leadership, innovation and spiritual strength. The future we desire must be intentionally built on them.
Weekly Activity
Discuss the lapses of Eli in raising his children and the consequences.
Prayer Points
  • Father, grant us wisdom and patience to raise godly children.
  • Father, plant Your fear, humility and divine virtues in my children.
  • Father, shield our children from ungodly associations.
  • Father, let Your word take deep roots in the lives of our children.
  • Father, give our children obedient hearts and ability to say no to sin.
