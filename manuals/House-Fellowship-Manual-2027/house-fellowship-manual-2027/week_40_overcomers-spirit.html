Week 40 — THANKSGIVING SUNDAY, 6TH JUNE, 2027

OVERCOMER'S SPIRIT

Memory Verse
“Ye are of God, little children, have overcome them: because greater is he that is in you, than he that is in the world. 1 John 4:4
Bible Text
John 16:33
Introduction
1 John 4:4 tells us that regardless of our age, level, background, tribe, race, etc., the moment we become God's children, we will overcome whatever comes our way because we are God's children. The Lord dwell in us and He is greater than whatever problem or challenge that may come our way. As believers, we are called to be overcomers through faith in Christ, who has already secured our victory over the challenges of life.
Lesson Outline One
BECOMING AN OVERCOMER
To become an overcomer is a decision every believer should make based on the word of God in 1 John 4:4. When you have made that decision, then you will work towards it by doing the following and many more: Relationship with Jesus. Matthew 11:28. Know who you are. Prov. 2:7. See yourself the way God sees you, don't give room for doubt. Jas 1:7. Intimacy with God. James 4:8. You cannot operate as an overcomer without intimacy with Him. Believe and declare the word. Numbers 14:28. Rev.12:11, Colossians 2:13-15. Act on the Word. Hebrew 10:38. Use your authority. Luke 10:19. Stand in faith. Ephesians 6:13-16.
Lesson Outline Two
CHARACTERISTICS OF AN OVERCOMER
The scriptures tell us that a tree is known by its fruits. Likewise, overcomers are known by the character and attitude they display anytime and wherever there are challenges. Some of the characteristics are: Faith in Jesus Christ. Daniel 11:32, Hebrews 10:38. Endurance through trials. Overcomers demonstrate resilience in the face of adversity. James 1:12. Recognizing ones identity in Christ. Romans 8:37, 1 John 5:4. Viewing challenges as stepping stones, opportunities for growth rather than obstacles. John 13:15.
Conclusion
Every believer has been given the authority to overcome all the challenges and tribulations of the world. John 16:33. The extent of your victories will be determined by how much your faith, the power you draw from Jesus. Matthew 28:18, the source of all power in your daily devotion, prayer life and obedience. John 5:39, John 14:13-14, 2 Corinthians 10:6.
Weekly Activity
Discuss five biblical characters that showed confidence in God in times of tribulations.
Prayer Points
  • Father, thank You for making me an overcomer.
  • Father, I make up my mind to walk with You daily, please help me to be faithful.
  • Father, reveal Yourself to me daily as I study your word.
  • Father, equip me with power to overcome the obstacles on my path to victory.
  • Father, make my life a testimony of Your completed work at Calvary.
