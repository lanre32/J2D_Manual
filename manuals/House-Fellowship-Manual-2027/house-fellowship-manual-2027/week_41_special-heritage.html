SPECIAL HERITAGE

Opening Prayer

Previous Knowledge
Leader are to remind the members on last lesson on overcomer's spirit.
Memory Verse
“I can do all things through Christ which strengthens me” Philippians 4:13
Bible Text
1 Samuel 2:9
Introduction
In Zechariah 2:8, God declared that we are the apple of His eyes and His heritage, whatever untoward affects us affects our God and He will rise to solve the problem. Hebrews 4:15. Isaiah 49:16 tells us that He has engraved us in the palm of His hands. What God needs from us is to know how dear we are to Him and trust Him to shower us with His love. Proverbs 3:5-6, Isaiah 26:4, Psalm 121:7-8. As we trust and hold on Him, we will continually enjoy His favour and blessings in every area of our lives. Numbers 6:24-26.
Lesson Outline One
HUMAN STRENGTH FAILS
Defeat of Israel by Ai in Joshua 7 shows us that human might can fail. When there is sin in the camp of believers defeat is inevitable. When you have divine presence, the giant must fall. 1 Samuel 17. It does not matter how tough, big, strong the enemy or the problem is, when God backs you up, you will laugh. Zechariah 4:6-7. Eyes of Most High watch over His children to defend, fight their battles, provide for them and bless them. Exodus 14:14, Isaiah 41:10-13. Believers are to accomplish their tasks and fulfil their destinies since God's presence is with them. We are special heritage of our God. 1 Peter 2:9, Philippians 4:13. Human being's ability is limited but God is limitless. Psalm 108:12.
Lesson Outline Two
UNDERSTANDING GOD'S STRENGTH AND BENEFITS
Understanding His strength is not just about knowing that He's powerful, it is about experiencing His power in our lives on daily basis, because we will always encounter challenges that test our physical, emotion, financial and intellectual capacity. To understand and enjoy the benefits of God's strength, we must know the following; God's strength is eternal and unshaking. Isaiah 40:28. Daniel 11:32 God renews our strength when we are weary. Isaiah 40:31 His strength is the refuge in times of trouble. Psalm 46:1 That there is provision of peace in the midst of chaos. John 14:27. There is boldness and courage to confront obstacles and challenges. 2 Timothy 1:7, Joshua 1:9.
Conclusion
God's strength is not just a concept, it is a living reality for those who trust Him. When we acknowledge our weakness, lean on his power, and walk in faith, we experience a life of resilience, peace and victory.
Weekly Activity
Discuss vivid biblical example of human strength that failed
