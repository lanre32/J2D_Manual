Week 42 — SUNDAY, 13TH JUNE, 2027

CONTROL YOUR APPETITE

Memory Verse
“For their god is their belly, and whose glory is in their shame” Philippians 3:19
Bible Text
Proverbs 23:1-3
Introduction
Appetite is not evil if it is positive and not character damaging or bringing insults to the body of Christ but lack of control is the problem. Uncontrolled appetite has destroyed destinies, homes, etc.
Lesson Outline One
UNDERSTANDING APPETITE AND TYPES
Appetite is a strong desire for pleasure, power, attention, food and approval. Proverbs 16:32 says he that ruleth his spirit is better than he that taketh a city. Examples of uncontrolled appetite; Esau. It caused him birthright. Genesis 25:29-34. Samson sold his strength, glory and his calling to indiscipline and insatiable quest for sex. Judges 16. Adam and Eve's desire for what God forbid caused them their inheritance. Genesis 3:6.
Lesson Outline Two
TYPES OF APPETITE AND DANGERS OF NOT CONTROLLING
There are uncontrolled appetite; Physical - Proverbs 23:20-21. Gluttony leads to sickness and laziness. Emotional - Seeking attention, depending on people. Jeremiah 17:5. Spiritual - having junks as food will lead to unfulfilled dreams. Matthew 4:4 WHAT ARE THE DANGERS OF UNCONTROLLED APPETITE? Loss of destiny and weakness in prayer life. Broken relationship. Spiritual imprisonment. Shame and Regret. Adam and Eve were sent out of garden of Eden.
Conclusion
Developing self-discipline, practicing spiritual exercise and feeding on undiluted word of God will reduce craving for fleshy things. Set boundaries on your appetite.
Weekly Activity
What are the virtues uncontrolled appetite can withdraw from the captives?
Prayer Points
  • Father, deliver me from destructive appetite.
  • Holy Spirit, empower me to walk in self-control.
  • Every appetite fighting my destiny be subdued.
  • I receive grace to hunger for righteousness.
Golden Diet For The Week
Monday Like a city whose walls are broken through is a person who lacks self-control. Proverbs 25:28
Tuesday The destiny of victim of uncontrolled appetite is destruction. Philippians 3:19.

Friday Make no provision for the flesh. Romans 13:14.

Sunday I discipline my body and bring it unto subjection. 1 Corinthians 9:27
