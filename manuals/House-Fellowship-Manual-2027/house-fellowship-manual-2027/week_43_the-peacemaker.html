Week 43 — SUNDAY, 20TH JUNE, 2027

THE PEACEMAKER

Memory Verse
“Blessed are the peacemakers; for they shall be called children of God” Matthew 5:9
Bible Text
Colossians 3:13-15
Introduction
Peace is not the absence of trouble or discontentment, it is the presence of God navigating His influence of establishing God's order. God does not only call us to have peace, but to make peace. A peace maker is not weak but a Christ inspired being.
Lesson Outline One
PEACEMAKER
A peacemaker is the one who; Promotes reconciliation. Exodus 32:11-14 Stops strife, not spreads it. Restores broken relationships. Luke 23:34 Reflects God's nature of peace over nations. Genesis 13, 18. Speaks truth in love. Ephesians 4:15.
Lesson Outline Two
QUALITIES OF A TRUE PEACEMAKER
Pure and gentle. James 3:17-18. Easy to be entreated. Full of mercy. Controls their tongue. Proverbs 15:1 Walks in humility. Proverbs 13:10. Chooses forgiveness. Colossians 3:13
Conclusion
Peacemakers are called children of God, enjoys divine favor, experiences peace and releases healing in families, communities, churches and nations. Psalm 34:14.
Weekly Activity
What are the dangers of dwelling in rancourous atmosphere?
Prayer Points
  • Father, make me an instrument of your peace.
  • Every spirit of strife in my life, be uprooted.
  • Grace to forgive and reconcile, rest upon me.
  • Let peace reign in my home and nation.
  • Lord, increase the strength and your grace on Daddy and Mummy GO.
Golden Diet For The Week
Monday Blessed are the peacemakers for they shall be called children of God. Matthew 5:9.
Tuesday If it is possible as far as it depends on you, live at peace with everyone. Romans 12:18.
Wednesday Make every effort to live in peace with everyone and to be holy. Hebrews 12:14.
Thursday A gentle answer turns away wrath, but a harsh word stirs up anger. Proverbs 15:1
Friday Bear with each other and forgive each other. Colossians 3:13-15.
Saturday Be completely humble and gentle, be patient bearing with one another. Ephesians 4:2-3
Sunday Turn from evil and do good, seek peace and pursue it. Psalm 34:14.
