Week 44 — SUNDAY, 27TH JUNE, 2027

THE DIVINE EDIT PART ONE

Memory Verse
“Wherefore seeing we also are compassed about with so great a cloud of witness, let us lay aside every weight and the sin which doth so easily beset us and let us run with patience the race that is before us” Hebrew 12:1.
Bible Text
Luke 10:38-42
Introduction
We live in an age of abundance - abundant information, choices, demands on our time and attention. Our lives often feel overflowing, overscheduled and overwhelmed. Just as a write edits a manuscript, a filmmaker edits footage, or a gardener prunes a plant we need a process of editing our lives.
Lesson Outline One
NEED FOR DIVINE EDIT
There is a saying that when a man recognizes and acknowledges that he has a problem, the problem is half solved, when we have clutter in our lives, it needs to be edited. A clutter is a collection of things lying about in an untidy state, our heart can be room that is cluttered. This obvious clutter- habits, attitudes, relationship that pull us away from God. These are not just distractions, they are hindrance. Good things can hinder like good activities too much comfort, good relationship can deprive God's relationship, good causes, or even good intentions can become clutter if they distract us from our primary calling or exhaust us to the point of spiritual depletion. Martha's dilemma in Luke 10:38-42.
Lesson Outline Two
SYMPTOMS OF A CLUTTERED LIFE
Feeling constantly busy but rarely productive in what truly matters. Spiritual dryness or inconsistency. Emotional exhaustion, anxiety or resentment. Lack of peace and clarity. Difficulty hearing God's voice. We are to fix our eyes on Jesus the pioneer and perfecter of faith. Hebrews 12:2. He is the ultimate example of a life perfectly edited, perfectly focused on the Father's will
Conclusion
What is one area of your life where you sense God is calling for edit this week? Invite God into that space, ask him to show you what needs to be thrown off like Jonah so that boat of your life won't sink.
Weekly Activity
Set a day apart this week for thorough examination of yourself.
Prayer Points
  • Father, purge my heart from hypocrisy, pride and hidden sin.
  • Father, surround me with godly and visionary friends and associates.
  • Lord, let my life, service and labour receive your approval and not rejection.
  • Father, banish from me glory draining adventures and associates.
  • Lord, fill Daddy and Mummy GO with fresh anointing and keep on renewing them.
