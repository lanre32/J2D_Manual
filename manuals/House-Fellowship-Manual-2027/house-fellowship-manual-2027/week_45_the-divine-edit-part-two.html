Week 45 — THANKSGIVING SUNDAY, 4TH JULY, 2027

THE DIVINE EDIT PART TWO

Memory Verse
“But seek ye first the kingdom of God and his righteousness and all these things shall be added unto you. Matthew 6:33” Hebrew 12:1
Bible Text
Exodus 18:13-26
Introduction
Last week we examined divine edit part one, focusing on the need to remove the clutter that fills our lives and the signs of a cluttered life. Today we will be explore the practical steps of a cluttered life and the tools needed for editing.
Lesson Outline One
PRACTICAL EVIDENCE IN A CLUTTERED LIFE
Looking at 1 kings 19:13 and Luke 15:11-32, these are steps of a cluttered life; Unused or abandoned virtues. 1 Corinthians 14:40. Overthinking and worry Unresolved decisions, negative thoughts and self-doubt. Lack of focus. Philippians 4:8. Unforgiveness, past hurts, guilt and shame. Hebrews 12:1 Over commitment, poor time management and saying yes to everything. No clear priority. Neglecting prayer altar, religious activities without relationship
Lesson Outline Two
EDITOR'S TOOLS
Jesus Christ is the divine editor, when we surrender to Him, He will edit our lives to fulfil His purpose. So let us glue our eyes to Him to effect positive transformation in our lives. Hebrews 12:2 The word of God. Hebrews 4:12. His word will help us to discern between good, better and the best. The Holy Spirit. The Spirit convicts, guides and illuminates. He brings clarity to confusion. Prayer and solitude. This is our editing session in quiet moment, laying our lives before God. Wise Counsel. Sometimes God uses trusted mentors, pastors or friends to help us see the clutter we have become blind to. Exodus 18:2'
Summary
When our lives are being edited, it's not about making our lives empty but making them full of what truly matters. It's about freedom, focus and fruitfulness, it's about running the race marked out for us with perseverance.
Conclusion
A cluttered life drains energy, blocks progress and steals peace, let divine editor work on you. Avoid toxic relationship, unnecessary conflicts and unhealthy boundary
Weekly Activity
What are the boundaries you ought to set to avoid cluttering?
Prayer Points
  • Father, grant me peace that silences every noise and confusion in my mind.
  • O Lord deliver me from anxiety, fear and mental overload and restore my sound mind.
  • I cast out everything cluttering my thought hindering me from hearing God's voice
  • Every negative harmful, unproductive thought pattern in my mind be uprooted.
  • Father, align my thought with your will.
