Week 46 — SUNDAY, 11TH JULY, 2027

DON'T BE CARELESS

Memory Verse
“Therefore we ought to give the more earnest heed to the things which we have heard lest at any time we should let them slip” Hebrew 2:1
Bible Text
Matthew 25:1-13
Introduction
Carelessness is acting without attention, discipline or reverence. Many people lose great things in life not because of Satan alone but because they are careless. Spiritual gifts, marriages, opportunities, health and destiny have been lost through carelessness.
Lesson Outline One
CARELESSNESS
A little yeast can be like carelessness does not shout, it whispers. It starts small but ends big. Samson was careless with his consecration and paid for it. Judges 16:17-21. Esau was careless with his birthright, he paid with groaning. Genesis 25:34. Eli was careless with discipline his sons, lost his ministry and lost them one day. 1 Samuel 2:29-30. Carelessness opens doors to losses, weakens spiritual life and ruin destiny. Matthew 26:41 Don't be careless with your prayer, bible study and consecration life. Never allow your lamp to go out.
Lesson Outline Two
HOW TO AVOID CARELESS LIVING?
Stay alert spiritually. 1 Peter 5:8 Value instruction and correction. Proverbs 12:1. Imbibe godly living. 1 Timothy 4:16. Put God first in your decisions. Eccl. 12:13. Avoid gathering of careless people. Proverbs 1:10 Examine your life regularly. 2 Corinthians 13:5
Conclusion
God rewards careful living. Learn from Joseph that ran away from being careless with his service at Potiphar's house. Proverbs 4:23, 1 Corinthians 15:58
Weekly Activity
Discuss other biblical characters that were careless and the consequences.
Prayer Points
  • Father, deliver me from every form of spiritual carelessness.
  • Father, guide my thought and decisions.
  • I reject any careless step that can lead me to give regret.
  • Father, help me to value and maximize opportunity.
  • Lord, deliver me from a careless prayer life, help me to remain consistent and devoted.
Golden Diet For The Week
