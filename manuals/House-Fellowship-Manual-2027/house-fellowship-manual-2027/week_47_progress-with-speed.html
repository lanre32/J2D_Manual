Week 47 — SUNDAY, 18TH JULY, 2027

PROGRESS WITH SPEED

Memory Verse
“Our soul is escaped as a bird out of the snare of the fowlers; the snare is broken and we are escaped” Psalm 124:7
Bible Text
Psalm 126:1-4
Introduction
Promotion can come to you either human or divine assistance. The assisted state of progress attained through this promotion moves you to the next level you are not supposed to be, may the Lord in this season assist you and move you to a level you are not qualified to occupy in Jesus name.
Lesson Outline One
ACTIONS TO ACTIVATE GOD KIND OF PROGRESS
Progress with speed does not mean rushing blindly it means carrying out actions to activate it. Align with God's plan and mandate. Be diligent, consistent and be obedience. Proverbs 21:5, Proverbs 22:29. Activate faith for acceleration. Matthew 17:20. Remove barriers that can slow you down. Always hunger for more. God satisfy hungry souls. Have visions and plans. Hab.2:3
Lesson Outline Two
DANGERS OF BEING STAGNANT
Where there is no vision, people will perish. Let us look at dangers of stagnation. It makes believer weak and vulnerable. Lot was weak in Sodom. Genesis 19. There will be missed opportunities. Ecclesiastes 11:4. Loss of influence. Matthew 5:13. Lack of spiritual growth makes believer vulnerable to temptation. Stagnation prevents one from fulfilling destiny. Jeremiah 29:11
Conclusion
God does not want us to just move slowly, He desires accelerated advancement in our lives being stagnant is even dangerous
Key Note
God wants you not just move forward but move forward swiftly.
Prayer Points
  • Father, I refuse stagnation, accelerate my progress.
  • Father, open doors of new opportunity for me in this year.
  • Lord, cut off every delay and limitation in my life, remove all obstacles on my path.
  • Father, strengthen Daddy and Mummy GO to walk diligently and remain consistent to divine call
  • Lord, let your favour lift me to the next level.
