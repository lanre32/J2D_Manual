Week 48 — SUNDAY, 25TH JULY, 2027

GO SIN NO MORE

Memory Verse
“She said, no man Lord. And Jesus said unto her, neither do I condemn thee: go and sin no more” John 8:11
Bible Text
John 8:3-11
Introduction
Sin is primarily any act that is against the laws, will, plan and purpose of God which encompasses our actions, our thoughts, words and omissions. Our God is pure and cannot tolerate sin. Hab. 1:13, Isaiah 59:1-2. Whenever we commit sin, we are challenging the Lordship of God over our lives and submitting to the ownership and authority of the devil over our lives. John 8:44, 1 John 3:8. Therefore, as children of God, we are expected to run away from sin. 1 Thessalonians 5:22 so that we can continue to enjoy the benefits of our sonship. However, when we fall into sin and we repent, God is merciful and will forgive us and restore us back to Himself. Proverbs 28:11
Lesson Outline One
GOD IS MERCIFUL. Psalm 103:8
His great love and compassion makes Him to release His mercy. Lam. 3:23. His mercy brings forgiveness of sin. Ephesians 2:4-5. God even feels our pains, shame and reproach. Psalm 34:18. His mercy overrules any form of judgement.
Lesson Outline Two
MERCY, NOT A LICENSE TO SIN
Mercy is not a license to sin rather God releases His mercy to give you another opportunity to consider your ways, repent and return to Him. Jeremiah 3:22, Romans 6:1. God's grace to overcome sin is released upon every Christian. 2 Corinthians 12:9, it is also sufficient to overcome all temptations of sin and to work out our salvation. Philippians 2:12. As Christians, God's mercy and grace makes us; Free from hold of sin. Romans 6:14. Possesses seed of righteousness. James 3:18. This seed of righteousness enables us to obey God's word and His instructions. Ephesians 5:6-10. We are alive to God. Galatians 2:19-20. God's grace enables us to run away from sin and to please God in everything we do. 1 Thess. 5:22
Conclusion
Sin is a destroyer of man because it leads to spiritual separation from God Isaiah 59:1-2 and without God man is useless. God's mercy is available to cleanse us from every form of sin and as we receive God's forgiveness and cleansing we are to run from anything that will make us fall into sin. 2 Corin. 5:17

Prayer Points
  • Father, I surrender my all to you please cleanse me from all unrighteousness
  • Father, please sanctify my heart by your blood and your word of truth.
  • Father, by your grace I will overcome the temptations of sin.
  • Father, please sow the seeds of righteousness in my heart.
  • Father, I promise to live my life to please You.
