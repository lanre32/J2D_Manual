Week 49 — SUNDAY, 8TH AUGUST, 2027

LOVE, THE FRUIT OF HOLY SPIRIT

Memory Verse
“and now abideth faith, hope, charity, these, but the greatest of these is charity” 1 Corin. 13:13
Bible Text
1 Corinthians 13:1-13
Introduction
Love is the fruit of the Holy Spirit, Galatians 5:22. When we love, it is evidence that the Spirit of God lives in us, 1 John 4:13, 16. He enables us to love like God loves. God manifested His love by sending Jesus Christ as a sacrifice to take away our sins. If God loves us this much, we ought to love each other not by our power but by power of the Holy Spirit who dwells in us. Anyone who loves is a child of God but anyone who does not love does not belong to God, for God is love. 1 John 4:7-8
Lesson Outline One
LOVE PERSONIFIED
God is love, He manifested His love in the Person of Jesus Christ to us. God sent His only begotten Son, Our Lord Jesus Christ to die for our sins - this is love personified. 1 John 3:9. When we receive God's love we receive the gift of eternal life. For God so loved us that He gave Jesus Christ to save us from our sins and free us from death. Whosoever believes in Him shall not perish but have everlasting life, John 3:16. When we confess that Jesus is the Son of God, we know and believe the love God has for us. 1 John 4:15-16. We become one in God - we dwell in God and God dwells in us and nothing shall be able to separate us from the love of God in Christ Jesus our Lord. Romans 8:7-39.
Lesson Outline Two
ESSENTIALS OF LOVE
God's love comprises of essentials which shows that the Holy Spirit is the Source. Hence the title of our lesson, love, the fruit of the Spirit. We find essentials of love in the Word of God. Love is patient and kind. 1 Corinthians 13:4 Love is not envious or jealous, proud or rude, provoked or thinks evil. 1 Corinthians 13:4-5 Love is neither irritable nor keeps record of being wronged. 1 Corinthians 13:5. Love rejoices in truth not in injustice. 1 Corinthians 13:6. Love never fails, is always hopeful, full of faith and lasts forever. 1 Corinthians 13:7. Love gives and is not self-seeking - 1 John 4:10. Love does not fear. 1 John 4:18. Love does not hate. 1 John 4:20.
Conclusion
Loving others is the evidence we must show that we know God and His Spirit dwells in us. If you say you love God but hate your brother, you lie. If you do not love your brother who you see, how can you love God who you have not seen? 1 John 4:20. God commands us to love one another as He has loved us.
Weekly Activity
What are the practical ways of loving others?
Prayer Points
  • Father, fill my heart with Your divine love through the Holy Spirit.
  • Lord, remove every hardness and bitterness from my heart.
  • Lord, let love govern my reactions and responses.
  • Every root of offense in my heart, be uprooted in Jesus Name.
  • Lord, make me a vessel through which Your love flows to others.
