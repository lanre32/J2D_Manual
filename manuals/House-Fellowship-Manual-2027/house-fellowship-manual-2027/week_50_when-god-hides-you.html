Week 50 — SUNDAY, 15TH AUGUST, 2027

WHEN GOD HIDES YOU

Memory Verse
“Get thee hence, and turn thee eastward, and hide thyself by the brook Cherith, that is before Jordan” 1 kings 17:3
Bible Text
Matthew 2:13-15
Introduction
It is wisdom to obey when He instruct you to hide. Known unto God are all His works from the foundation of the world. He will keep in perfect peace those whose minds are stayed on Him because they trust in Him, Isaiah 26:3. There is time for everything, Ecclesiastes 3:1-11. Our Bible text shows us that God the Father instructed Joseph to hide our Lord Jesus Christ from Herod who sought to kill Him. Joseph obeyed God's instruction that Herod was dead, Matthew 2:19-20. When God hides you, you must remain in hiding and obediently wait for your time of showing.
Lesson Outline One
THERE IS TIME TO HIDE
We see through scriptures that there is time a to hide. There are several accounts in the bible where God instructed people to hide and those who obeyed Him were preserved. Some examples are: 1. In the days of Noah, God commanded Noah and His family to enter the ark to preserve them from destruction. Genesis 7:1 2. He commanded the children of Israel to hide in their houses marked with blood on the lintel while the destroyer passed over their houses. Exodus 12:23. 3. He commanded Elijah to hide in a place to be fed during a famine. 1 kings 17:2-3. 4. He commanded Joseph to hide the child Jesus Christ until those who sought to kill him were dead. Matthew 2:13-20. There were also accounts in the Bible where God gave people wisdom to either hide or hide others from danger and they were preserved. Examples are: Rahab hid the two spies. Joshua 2:3-15. David hid from King Saul. 1 Samuel 22:1 Obadiah hid one hundred prophets. 1 kings 18:4 Mordecai hid Esther's identity. Esther 2:10. Lot and his family were removed from destruction of Sodom and Gomorrah. Genesis 19:15-22 Israelites were hidden in Goshen from plagues. Exodus 8:22, 9:26, 10:23
Lesson Outline Two
TIME TO HIDE IS GOD'S PEROGATIVE
Timing is in God's hands, He is the righteous judge. Genesis 18:25. God hides people only at His appointed time. Isaiah 26:20. God's action is effected when His plan is completed. Genesis 7:1. Preparation is man's responsibility, timing is God's authority. Deliverance will surely come at the appointed time. Exodus 12:41. Early or late movement in action can lead to destruction. Numbers 14:42-45. Acting outside God's timing - even with good intentions - leads to loss. God's protection operates by divine schedule. Ecclesiastes 3:1. There is time to stay, hide and escape
Conclusion
God's prerogative is not just to save, but to decide when salvation is activated. Come my people enter thou into thy chambers and shut thy doors about thee: hide thyself as it were for a little moment until the indignation be overpast. Isaiah 26:20
Weekly Activity
What are the ways and when is the time children of God can discern that it is time to hide?
Prayer Points
  • Father, thank you for teaching us that there is time to hide.
  • Father, please keep me in Your secret place in Jesus Name.
  • Father, please hide me under the shadow of Your wings.
  • Father, reveal to Your children when it is time to hide and show them how to stay safe in Jesus Name.
  • Father, please keep our parents in the Lord as the apple of Your eyes and hide them under the shadow of your wings in Jesus name.
