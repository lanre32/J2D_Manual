RCCG House Fellowship Manual

Week 51 — SUNDAY, 22ND AUGUST, 2027

HOME OF THE RIGHTEOUS

Memory Verse
“The wicked are overthrown, and are not: but the house of the righteous shall stand.” Proverbs 12:7
Bible Text
Genesis 18:19
Introduction
The righteous keeps the way of the Lord to do justice and judgement and this is more acceptable of the Lord than sacrifice, Proverbs 21:3. Abraham became a mighty nation, and all nations of the earth are blessed in him just as the Lord promised because God judged him faithful. Romans 4:17-18. God blessed Abraham because He knew Abraham will teach his children to live righteously, to do justice and judgement. This was the guarantee that God's blessing will continue from generation to generation, Genesis 18:19. Justice and judgement are the habitation of God, Psalm 89:14. Therefore God dwells in the home of the righteous.
Lesson Outline One
WHAT MAKES A HOME RIGHTEOUS?
God dwells in the home of the righteous. John 14:23. The word of God is taught and obeyed. Genesis 18:19 The parents teach their children the word of God. Deuteronomy 6:7. The father walks in integrity and his children are blessed after him. Proverbs 20:7. The husband loves his wife and is not bitter against her. Colossians 3:19. The wife submits to her husband as it is fit in the Lord. Colossians 3:18. The children obey their parents in all things: for this is well pleasing in the Lord. Colossians 3:20. The father does not provoke his children lest they be discouraged. Col 3:21. The workers serve heartly with sincerity and in the fear of the Lord Acts 9:31, Colossians 3:22. The blessing and favour of God. Psalm 5:12
Lesson Outline Two
BLESSINGS OF HOME OF THE RIGHTEOUS
The presence of God dwells there. Proverbs 3:33. They enjoy calmness, order and divine stability. Isaiah 32:18. Evil plans and destructive forces are restrained by God's edge. Psalm 121:7. God supplies needs and blesses the work of their hands. Proverbs 15:6. Children and descendants benefit from the righteousness of the parents. Psalm 112:2. The atmosphere of the home is filled with gladness and thanksgiving. Proverbs 29:6. The home is preserved and established over time. Proverbs 12:7
Conclusion
Nevertheless the foundation of God standeth sure, having this seal, the Lord knoweth them that are His. And, let everyone that nameth the Name of Christ depart from iniquity. 2 Timothy 2:19
Weekly Activity
Discuss how parents can practically ensure their children are taught the word of God.
Prayer Points
  • Father, thank you for teaching us that the home of the righteous is blessed and that righteousness is profitable.
  • Father, help me to live righteously and walk before You in integrity.
  • Father, teach me to number my days and apply my heart unto wisdom.
  • Father, give all parents the wisdom to train their children in Your way in Jesus name.
  • Father, let our parents in the Lord enjoy the rewards of their labour of love over us.
