Session Notes
LOVE FEAST/ THANKSGIVING/ EVALUATION OF THE JUST ENDED YEAR AND DELIBERATIONS ON HOW TO MOVE OUR CENTERS FORWARD. a. Leader and member should discuss challenges confronting the centre. b. Suggestions that can foster growth of house fellowship. c. The materials used this year, what is of quality or not? d. Is the environment where the centre is conducive or manageable? e. Center should organize love feast. The leader should write down suggestion for review and later bless centre members
Prayer Points
  • Father, thank you for carrying us through another RCCG calendar year.
  • Lord, as another RCCG year ends, everything assigned to end my year in shame be silenced.
  • Every last minute sorrow, evil agenda is cancelled over my life.
  • O God of completion complete every good thing you started in my life.
  • What was delayed shall be released, what was pending for me shall be settled.
